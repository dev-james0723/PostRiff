# Breaking-news rapid response

## Purpose

Create a timely first draft after authoritative confirmation without pretending the full meaning or real-world performance is already known.

## Required inputs

- Primary-source confirmation or an explicitly attributed rumor-analysis request.
- Two to four material claims.
- Unknowns and excluded claims.
- James's real first observation or question.

## Structure

1. What happened, in one or two sentences.
2. The few confirmed facts that materially change the story.
3. James's observation, clearly separated from fact.
4. What remains untested, unavailable, disputed, or uncertain.
5. One useful source or next thing to watch.

## Transformation notes

- X, Threads, Bluesky, or Mastodon: one strong idea; use a thread only when sequence is essential.
- LinkedIn: add the builder, creator, or industry implication.
- Instagram or Facebook: use a visual only when it adds evidence, explanation, or a strong editorial frame.
- YouTube Community: offer one useful observation or genuine question beyond an announcement.
- Xiaohongshu or Weibo: localize framing and vocabulary; do not translate the global caption mechanically.

## Validation

- No `breaking`, `just announced`, or similar urgency after the story-age window expires.
- No implication that James tested, heard, used, or measured the subject without evidence.
- Rumor repetition never becomes confirmation.
- The draft creates no publish job by itself.
