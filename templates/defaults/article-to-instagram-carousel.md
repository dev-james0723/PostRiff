# Article to Instagram carousel

## Purpose

Turn one article thesis into a phone-readable visual sequence. The carousel extracts one argument; it does not compress every article section onto slides.

## Required inputs

- Article or approved canonical brief.
- One thesis and intended audience.
- Claim references and required qualifications.
- James's point of view.
- Current Instagram format constraints and available media.

## Slide roles

Choose only the roles needed by the idea:

1. **Cover:** a true, specific tension or promise.
2. **Context:** the minimum background needed to understand it.
3. **Evidence:** one claim or relationship per slide.
4. **Implication:** what changes in practice or understanding.
5. **James's view:** interpretation distinguished visually from fact.
6. **Limitation:** what remains uncertain or does not follow from the evidence.
7. **Landing:** conclusion or useful next step; CTA only when earned.

The number of slides follows the argument. Do not pad to a fixed count.

## Caption

- Open with a complementary line rather than repeating the cover.
- Supply context that does not fit comfortably on the cards.
- Preserve sources and qualifications.
- Use restrained hashtags only when they improve discovery.
- One CTA at most, and no automatic `save/share/agree` language.

## Visual direction

- Use the selected brand identity and eligible visual template.
- Maintain one reading hierarchy and rhythm without repeating one rigid layout.
- Keep essential text legible in a phone-size preview.
- Recompose rather than blindly crop from article covers or other platforms.
- Return ordered asset IDs, text transcript, alt text, rights notes, and validation.

## Validation

- One idea per slide and no paragraph dumping.
- Cover claim matches the article evidence.
- Slide order creates a coherent argument.
- Localization reflows independently.
- Carousel and caption remain candidate-only until exact approval.
