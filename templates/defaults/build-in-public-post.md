# Build-in-public post

## Purpose

Turn a real product decision, failed attempt, trade-off, or small breakthrough into a useful post without becoming a feature-list announcement.

## Structure

1. The concrete problem James noticed.
2. The constraint or tension that made it difficult.
3. The decision or experiment.
4. Evidence of the current result, including what failed.
5. What changed in James's thinking.
6. What remains unfinished.
7. One useful next step or invitation when relevant.

## Platform adaptation

- LinkedIn: emphasize the product or working-method implication.
- X or Threads: isolate the strongest decision or tension.
- Instagram: use real UI, annotated evidence, or a concise carousel only when visual explanation helps.
- Xiaohongshu: localize as a practical build note, not a translated founder announcement.
- Reddit or Dcard: begin with community usefulness and disclose affiliation.

## Validation

- No roadmap item described as shipped.
- No invented user response, customer quote, metric, or outcome.
- Screenshot environment and product state are accurately labelled.
- The post still makes sense without a generic motivational conclusion.
