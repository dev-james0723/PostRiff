# Article to Xiaohongshu note

## Purpose

Transform an article into a natural Simplified-Chinese knowledge or opinion note for the exact Xiaohongshu account. Reframe for the audience instead of translating an Instagram or English caption.

## Required inputs

- Article or approved canonical brief.
- Exact account, audience, and current Xiaohongshu constraints.
- Confirmed facts, qualifications, and source references.
- James's real angle.
- Selected mode: `knowledge_note`, `opinion_note`, or `visual_diary`.

## Structure

1. **Cover:** concrete Chinese title with no fake urgency.
2. **Opening:** why this matters to the selected audience now.
3. **Context:** explain necessary terms in natural Chinese.
4. **Evidence or explanation:** use short sections or one idea per card.
5. **James's view:** distinguish personal interpretation from sourced fact.
6. **Trade-off or unknown:** preserve uncertainty and avoid product hype.
7. **Landing:** practical implication or honest question when useful.

## Localization rules

- Write in natural Simplified Chinese by default.
- Localize examples, rhythm, terminology, cover density, and CTA.
- Do not intensify a claim during translation.
- Avoid fake diary voice, invented personal testing, viral formula copying, and excessive emoji or hashtags.
- Retain source provenance internally; cite visibly when the claim or platform context requires it.

## Visual rules

- A knowledge note may use a cover plus explanatory cards.
- An opinion note may use a calmer editorial cover and fewer cards.
- A real product screenshot remains real evidence; generated imagery must not imitate official UI or documentary proof.
- Do not inherit Instagram card order automatically.

## Validation

- Current account and format constraints checked.
- Mainland localization reviewed.
- Title, body, tags, ordered media, originality status, and visibility remain exact approval fields.
- Draft-only when route, rights, evidence, or localization remains unresolved.
