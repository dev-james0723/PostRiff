# Calm editorial visual family

## Purpose

Provide a reusable visual direction for James Au content where thoughtful editorial clarity is more appropriate than promotional energy.

## Visual system

- Mood: calm, intelligent, human, contemporary, slightly tactile.
- Composition: meaningful negative space, one focal point, clear reading order.
- Palette: restrained neutrals with one content-driven accent; exact tokens come from the selected brand identity.
- Typography: one dominant headline level and at most one subordinate explanatory level on covers.
- Imagery: real photos, product evidence, score or studio detail, scientific/system imagery, or clearly conceptual generated editorial art.
- Rhythm: vary scale and silence across a carousel while retaining family resemblance.
- Texture: subtle paper, material, photographic, notation, or interface detail when relevant; never decorative noise by default.

## Responsive compositions

- Portrait feed: subject and headline balanced for 4:5 viewing.
- Square: recompose focal scale and line breaks; do not center-crop blindly.
- Landscape/article: thesis-led cover with room for link-card crops.
- Vertical cover: protect platform UI zones and keep the spoken/video hook distinct.
- Carousel: alternate evidence, explanation, and reflection layouts without losing hierarchy.

## Guardrails

- Do not imitate Apple, Instagram, another creator, or any external brand design system.
- Do not use corporate blue, gradients, dark mode, giant numerals, emojis, or rounded cards automatically.
- Do not fabricate official product renders, UI, scientific images, quotes, metrics, or event photography.
- Do not put the full caption on the image.
- Use no image when the visual adds no meaning.

## Required output metadata

- Visual thesis and brand identity.
- Native format and current constraint version.
- All visible text in reading order.
- Source/rights notes and generated-content status.
- Alt text and phone-size preview result.
- Mechanical, visual, factual, localization, accessibility, privacy, and rights validation.
