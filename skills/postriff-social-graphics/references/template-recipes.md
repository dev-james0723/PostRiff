# Template recipes by content mode

Templates define information flow, not a fixed visual style. Change composition, imagery, pacing, and language to fit the actual idea and destination.

## 1. Current news and opinion

Use only after the source log distinguishes confirmed, attributed, disputed, unknown, corrected, and retracted claims.

### Rapid visual

- Cover: the event plus the creator's actual question or observation.
- Fact card: two to four confirmed changes; cite the primary source.
- Tension card: what the announcement improves versus what remains unresolved.
- Landing: what the creator will watch, test, or think about next without implying firsthand use.

Never make generated editorial art look like an official product image or news photograph. Corrections invalidate affected text-bearing assets.

### Considered visual essay

Use an evidence → implication → creator's view → limitation sequence. A long-form article cover should express the thesis, not repeat the news headline.

## 2. Build in public

- Opening: the practical problem noticed.
- Evidence: real screenshot, diagram, test result, or failed attempt.
- Decision: the trade-off the creator made.
- Result: only what is actually demonstrated.
- Reflection: what changed in the creator's thinking or what remains open.

Prefer annotated UI, flow diagrams, changelog cards, before/current-state comparisons, and real workspace detail. Do not invent usage metrics or customer reactions.

## 3. Product launch and education

### Launch visual family

- Problem, expressed concretely.
- Product in a real use case.
- What a person can do now.
- One or two differentiating decisions.
- What is still in progress.
- One concrete next action.

Use real product UI whenever the post makes a factual interface or availability claim. Generated imagery can supply atmosphere but cannot replace proof of the shipped product.

### Tutorial carousel

- Outcome-first cover.
- Starting state or prerequisite.
- One action or decision per slide.
- Result and limitation.
- Optional next step.

Do not force a five-tip or seven-slide count.

## 4. YouTube release campaign

The thumbnail, Community Post visual, Shorts cover, Instagram carousel, and article cover have different jobs.

- Thumbnail: one tension, face/object hierarchy, minimal text, legible at small size.
- Community Post: give the audience a useful idea or question beyond "new video out."
- Short/Reel cover: identify the clip's own hook, not merely the long-video title.
- Carousel: explain one argument or sequence from the video.
- Link/article visual: communicate the durable thesis.

Do not publish a static image as if it were a completed video derivative.

## 5. Music, performance, and teaching

Prefer real performance photos, score details, instrument/studio textures, rehearsal frames, program context, and diagrams that make listening or technique clearer.

Possible structures:

- musical question → score/performance evidence → listening insight;
- practice problem → adjustment → what changed;
- performance moment → emotional or technical tension → reflection;
- composer/work context → one audible detail → invitation to listen.

Avoid craft elitism, fake quotations, generic poster grandeur, and visual claims that the creator performed, taught, or achieved something without evidence.

## 6. Personal reflection and everyday creative life

Use a real photo or understated generated editorial visual only when it adds emotional context. The graphic should leave space for ambiguity and should not convert vulnerability into motivational branding.

Useful forms:

- one precise observation over a quiet photograph;
- a two-panel tension rather than a triumphant before/after;
- a visual diary sequence with concrete details;
- a small object, room, score, interface, or routine as the anchor.

Do not invent the event, feeling, relationship, health condition, or lesson.

## 7. Educational explainer, framework, or comparison

Choose the visual structure from the relationship:

- sequence → timeline or numbered flow;
- alternatives → comparison table;
- hierarchy → tree;
- system → map or flowchart;
- repeated fields → matrix;
- one striking value → data card with source;
- concept plus examples → carousel.

Use source attribution for factual data. Do not create decorative charts without meaningful axes, units, or evidence.

## 8. Event, festival, studio, or service communication

Use the correct brand identity. Show real date, time, timezone, venue/location, eligibility, price, registration state, and CTA only when verified.

- Service or studio identity: warm photographic proof of the space or service, and a practical CTA.
- Institution or event identity: welcoming community imagery with clear logistics.
- Personal identity: why the creator cares or is involved, without turning every post into an institutional announcement.

Do not fabricate sold-out status, scarcity, partners, awards, or testimonials.

## 9. Community-native support asset

For Reddit, Dcard, Zhihu, Discord, Telegram, Feishu/Lark, QQ, LINE, KakaoTalk, WhatsApp Channels, or Google Business Profile, begin with the exact destination and purpose.

Use an asset only if it improves understanding, proves something, or supplies required event/product context. Avoid recycled advertisement layouts, unexplained campaign branding, engagement bait, or public-feed CTAs that conflict with community norms.
