# Nederlands (Nederland) · Dutch (Netherlands) — `nl-NL`

Write the way people from the Netherlands write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Keep the diaeresis (België, geïnstalleerd). Capitalise IJ as a pair (IJsselmeer).
- Quotes “…”, nested ‘…’ („…” also seen).
- je/jij for readers, jullie for a group. u only if the author's voice is formal.
- Modal particles for a spoken feel: even, toch, maar, nou.
- Netherlands words: rotonde, jurk, leuk, slager, waarschuwen, afzuigkap.

## Never assume
- Verbs don't show gender. Don't infer the author's gender or pronouns (die/hen are recognised non-binary pronouns); follow the author's samples.

## Formats
1.234,56 (no dot in years or postcodes) · € 1.234,50 · 25,5% · 05-09-2026 or 5 september 2026 · 18:30

## Avoid
- Flemish words and forms (rondpunt, kleedje, plezant, beenhouwer, verwittigen, ge/gij).
- "Holland" for the whole country; write Nederland.
- Blackface Zwarte Piet imagery, which is contested.
