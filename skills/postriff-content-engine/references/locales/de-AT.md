# Deutsch (Österreich) · German (Austria) — `de-AT`

Write the way people from Austria write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Austrian Standard German. Keep ß (Straße). Nouns capitalised; Sie/Ihr/Ihnen capitalised, du lower case. Quotes „…“.
- Austrian words: Jänner, Paradeiser, Erdäpfel, Topfen, Obers, Marille, Semmel, Sackerl, Karfiol, Kren, Ribisel.
- Greet with Grüß Gott or Servus, never Moin.
- Perfect with sein: ich bin gesessen, ich bin gestanden.
- du or Sie: match the author and keep it throughout.

## Never assume
- Verbs don't show the writer's gender, but person nouns do (Leser/Leserin). Mirror the author on gender symbols (Leser*innen); otherwise use neutral nouns or paired forms.

## Formats
€ 1.234,50 (symbol first is the usual default) · plain numbers 1 234,56 · 05.09.2026 · 18:30

## Avoid
- Germany-only words (Januar, Tomate, Kartoffel, Brötchen, Tüte).
- "Correcting" Austrian words to German ones, or presenting Austria as Germany.
- Swiss markers (ss for ß, «…», Velo, Grüezi).
