# 한국어 · Korean — `ko-KR`

Write the way people from South Korea write their own social posts in this variety. These are general conventions for the variety, not facts about the author: the author's VOICE.md decides tone and habits, and the approved facts decide content.

## Decisive markers
- Hangul with spaces between word units, particles attached (사진을). Spacing can change meaning; branded posts space correctly even though texting relaxes it.
- Western punctuation. Books in 《》 or 『』; films and other works in 〈〉 or 「」.
- One speech level per post: 해요체 (-어요) as the warm default, 합쇼체 (-습니다) for formal notices.
- Endings carry nuance: -네요 (discovery), -더라고요 (recounting), -잖아요 (shared knowledge), -죠 (checking agreement).
- Humble self-reference 저, 제, 저희; readers as 여러분.
- ㅋㅋ (bright laughter), ㅎㅎ (softer smile), ㅠㅠ and a trailing ~ warm casual text.

## Words that give the region away
| Write | Not |
|---|---|
| 셀카 | 자기 사진 (formal, stiff on social) |
| 핸드폰 | 휴대 전화 (formal register) |
| 파이팅 | 힘내세요 (formal; 파이팅 reads native) |
| 강추 (casual voice) | 적극 추천합니다 (formal register) |
| 저, 저희 to readers | 나, 우리 (not humble toward an audience) |
| -세요 | -십시오 (stiffer; modern voice uses -세요) |
| -어요 / -습니다 | -어 / -음 (반말 or 음슴체; only if the samples use it) |
| ㅋㅋ / ㅎㅎ | a lone ㅋ (can read as a sneer) |

## Tone, particles and address
- Open self-promotion easily reads as bragging (자랑). Frame it as sharing, credit others and thank: 부족하지만 열심히 준비했어요.
- Registers: 합쇼체 (formal, strangers), 해요체 (warm polite), 반말 (close friends only), 음슴체 (terse, online communities). Authors and brands default to 해요체; notices may use 합쇼체.
- Use ㅋㅋ, ㅎㅎ and ~ lightly, in casual voices only.
- Naver Blog: long, conversational paragraphs broken up around photos.

## Language mixing
- Hangul loanwords and Konglish coinages feel native (셀카, 파이팅).
- English in Latin script mostly in hashtags and brand names.

## Never assume
- Pronouns have no gender, but kin address does: 오빠 vs 형, 언니 vs 누나 depend on the speaker's gender. Never infer or assign the author's gender or age from them.
- Speech level toward readers follows the author's samples; otherwise 해요체 and 여러분.
- Don't assume the author's age, relationship or location.

## Formats
- Dates: 2026. 9. 16. (spaces, final period) or 9월 16일(수).
- Times: 오후 7시 or 19:00.
- Prices: 30,000원; 3만 원 for round figures.

## Local rules to flag, not to enforce
- Sponsored content must state the economic relationship in the title or opening lines, not only at the end (revised December 2024): if a disclosure is missing or buried, add a warning for the author.
- KakaoTalk Channel ad messages need "(광고)" and opt-out details: if missing, add a warning for the author.

## Avoid
- Mixing speech levels within one post.
- Spacing errors in branded posts.
- #소통 and #맞팔 in professional posts (follow-for-follow signal).
- Slang tied to gender conflict or politics; regional stereotypes.
