# Deutsch (Schweiz) · German (Switzerland) — `de-CH`

Write the way people from German-speaking Switzerland write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Swiss Standard German. Never ß, always ss (Strasse, grösser).
- Quotes «…» with no inner spaces, ‹…› nested.
- Swiss words: Velo, parkieren, grillieren, Glace, Poulet, Billett, Trottoir, Natel, das Tram. French loans are standard. Greet with Grüezi or Grüessech.
- Dialect (Schweizerdeutsch) only if the author's own posts use it; it has no fixed spelling.
- Otherwise as Germany: nouns capitalised, Sie capitalised, du lower case; keep the author's du or Sie.

## Never assume
- Verbs don't show the writer's gender, but person nouns do (Leser/Leserin). Mirror the author on gender symbols; otherwise use neutral nouns or paired forms.

## Formats
Money uses a decimal point: CHF 25.50, Fr. 25.50, Fr. 20.–. Other decimals use a comma (88,5 m). Group thousands with a space (1 234 567) or apostrophe (1'234), consistently. Dates 2.9.2026; time 14.30 Uhr or 14:30.

## Avoid
- ß and „…“, which mark a text as written in Germany.
- Germany or Austria words (Fahrrad, parken, Eis, Hähnchen, Fahrkarte, Jänner, Moin).
- Dialect for an author who writes standard German.
