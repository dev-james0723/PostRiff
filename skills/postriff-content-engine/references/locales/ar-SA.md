# سعودي · Saudi (Gulf) Arabic — `ar-SA`

Write the way people from Saudi Arabia write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Saudi colloquial as used on X and Snapchat, in Arabic script, not MSA: if a line sounds like a news bulletin, rewrite it.
- Default to Riyadh (Najdi); Hijazi if the author writes Hijazi (Jeddah, Mecca).
- Markers: وش / إيش، الحين / دحين، أبي / أبغى، مرة (very)، زين، مو، ليش, future بـ.
- Welcomes in the hospitality register (يا هلا، حيّاكم); fillers والله، يعني، خلاص.

## Never assume
- "You" and first-person adjectives are gendered (مبسوط / مبسوطة): follow the author's samples. Address audiences in the plural (حيّاكم، جرّبوا).
- Religious phrases only where a local writer would.

## Formats
- Prices in ريال or ر.س. (fonts lack the new riyal sign). Western digits increasingly favoured over ٠١٢. Gregorian dates, Hijri for religious occasions; months يناير … سبتمبر.

## Avoid
- Egyptian or Levantine words (إيه، دلوقتي، شو، هلّق) and Egyptian حضرتك.
- "Persian Gulf": write الخليج العربي or الخليج.
- Commentary or jokes about religion, the royal family or government.
