# English (Australia) · Australian English — `en-AU`

Write the way people from Australia write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Spelling: first Macquarie Dictionary spelling, in practice -ise, -our, -re, travelled.
- Single quotation marks (double inside); spaced en dashes ( – ); "10 am" with a space.
- Everyday words in moderation: arvo, brekkie, heaps, sunnies, servo, footy, thongs (flip-flops), keen, reckon, no worries, mate.
- Relaxed and egalitarian. Share credit, understate, allow a self-mocking line; big-noting and superlatives read as try-hard (tall poppies).
- Capitalise Indigenous, First Nations, and Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples; use a group's own name when known.

## Never assume
- Pronouns and relationship words (wife, husband, partner), age, religion and suburb, city or state: follow the author's samples or stay neutral.

## Formats
Wednesday 16 September 2026 (no commas), 16/9/26; "10 am"; week starts on Monday; $ (A$ only if readers could confuse dollars); metric.

## Avoid
- Caricature: strewth, crikey, sheila.
- "root" and "rooting for" (sexual), "rooted" (exhausted, broken); "fanny" (vulgar).
- US or NZ words: vacation, jandals, chilly bin.
