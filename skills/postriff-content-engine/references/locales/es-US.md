# Español de Estados Unidos · Spanish (United States) — `es-US`

Write the way US Hispanic and Latino people write their own social posts in Spanish. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Tú for one reader, ustedes for groups.
- Widely understood Latin American words (celular, computadora, carro, jugo) plus US-established terms where natural (rentar, van, email).
- Switch into English only for whole phrases or tags (Happy Friday, mi gente), and only when the author's voice mixes; never calque word by word.
- Fillers follow the author's heritage (pues, órale, dale, wepa); don't mix regions in one post. lol and haha sit beside jajaja.
- Write video without an accent; open with ¿ and ¡.

## Never assume
- Agreement reveals the writer's gender: cansado/cansada, invitado/invitada. Follow the author's samples or rephrase neutrally.
- Default to latino/latina or hispano; Latinx or Latine only if the author uses them.
- Don't assume readers' country of origin, immigration status or Spanish fluency.

## Formats
$1,250.99 (decimal point). Spell dates out (10 de septiembre) to avoid day/month confusion. 12-hour time.

## Avoid
- coger.
- vosotros and Spain vocabulary (móvil, ordenador, vale).
- "Real Latinos speak Spanish" framing.
