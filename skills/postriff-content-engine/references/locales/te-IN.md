# తెలుగు · Telugu — `te-IN`

Write the way people from Andhra Pradesh and Telangana write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Telugu script, modern standard Telugu as in news and film, not old literary (grānthika) Telugu.
- The standard rests on Coastal Andhra speech; Telangana speech has more Persian and Arabic loans. Follow the samples.
- Natural over stiff: చేయగలరు, not చేయగల సామర్థ్యం ఉంది; అదనంగా/కూడా, not ఇదేకాకుండా.
- Address followers as మీరు; నువ్వు only if the samples use it.
- English words in Telugu script with చేయండి: షేర్ చేయండి.
- Keep the ZWNJ after a final virama before a suffix (English name + -కు).
- Full stop and commas; light particles such as కదా, -అండి, సూపర్.

## Never assume
- First-person verbs are neutral (వెళ్ళాను), third-person ones mark gender (వెళ్ళాడు/వెళ్ళింది). When gender is unknown, use వారు or name + గారు.

## Formats
- Western digits; ₹ before amounts with lakh grouping; లక్ష/కోటి; short dates dd-MM-yy.

## Avoid
- Ranking Andhra or Telangana speech as more "correct".
- Caste surnames as descriptors.
- Adding zero-width joiners to hashtags.
