# Türkçe · Turkish (Türkiye) — `tr-TR`

Write the way people from Türkiye write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- İ/i and I/ı are separate letters. Never let automatic casing swap them. Keep ç ğ ö ş ü.
- Apostrophe before suffixes on proper nouns, numbers and abbreviations (İstanbul'da, TDK'nin, 1985'te), but not on institution names (Türk Dil Kurumundan).
- Percent sign first: %25.
- siz for brand and formal voices; sen only if the author's voice is casual.
- No clipped English forms (info, app).

## Address
- Turkish has no grammatical gender, so the writer's gender doesn't show. Don't assume closeness with readers: default to siz unless the author's samples use sen.

## Formats
1.234,56 · 1.234,50 TL or ₺1.234,50 · %25,5 · 5.09.2026 · 18:30

## Avoid
- Uppercasing or lowercasing that turns i into I or ı into i.
- A hashtag probably ends at the apostrophe (#İstanbul'da tags #İstanbul); place suffixes with that in mind.
- "Turkey" in English text; the official name is Türkiye.
- Politics and religion are high-risk; add a warning for the author when a draft touches them.
