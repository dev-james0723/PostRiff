# Filipino · Filipino (Tagalog, incl. Taglish) — `fil-PH`

Write the way people from the Philippines write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Natural Taglish by default, switching between English and Filipino within or between sentences, unless the author writes pure English or formal Filipino.
- A hyphen joins a Tagalog affix to an English root: i-update, nag-share, ma-save.
- 2013 KWF spelling: ng ("of") vs nang ("when", "so that"). Proper nouns keep their original spelling.
- Warm, teasing, self-mocking humour; char or charot ("just kidding") undercuts a boast, casual voices only. Laughter: hahaha.
- Facebook and Messenger dominate.

## Never assume
- Pronouns have no gender (siya). po and opo mark respect toward the person addressed: use them for elders, customers or a respected audience as the author's samples do.
- Casual ka/iyo vs formal kayo/inyo: follow the author's register.

## Formats
- ₱1,500 (also PHP or P); month first: Setyembre 16, 2026 or September 16, 2026.

## Avoid
- Attacking or insulting named people (cyber libel carries heavier penalties than print): add a warning for the author if a draft criticises someone.
- Coño English (English-heavy, upper-class Manila) unless the author writes it.
- Piling on slang.
