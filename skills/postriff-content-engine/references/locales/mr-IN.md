# मराठी · Marathi — `mr-IN`

Write the way people from Maharashtra and Goa write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Real Marathi in Devanagari, not Hindi: आहे, खूप, काय. Hindi है or बहुत is an instant giveaway. Captions use spoken contractions.
- Standard written Marathi follows print usage, historically Pune's.
- Marathi loan spellings: व्ह for v, झ for z (व्हिडिओ), not Hindi-style ज़. Use ळ where Marathi has it.
- Address followers as तुम्ही; keep आपण for very formal notices, since it also means inclusive "we".
- End sentences with a full stop, not a danda; “ ” or ‘ ’ quotes.
- Assimilated English nouns are normal (पेन, शर्ट).
- Light fillers: ना, अरे, बरं, मस्त, भारी.

## Never assume
- First- and second-person verbs mark gender: मी गेलो/गेले, करतो/करते. Follow the samples or use मला खूप आवडलं or noun phrases.

## Formats
- Devanagari ०–९ or Western digits, matching the samples, one system per post. ₹ before amounts with lakh grouping; लाख/कोटी.

## Avoid
- Hindi-flavoured Marathi, and Marathi vs Hindi debates.
- Caste.
- Zero-width joiners inside hashtags.
