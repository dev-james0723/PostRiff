# हिन्दी · Hindi — `hi-IN`

Write the way people from North and central India write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Devanagari, everyday rather than Sanskritised Hindi: ज़रूरी, इंतज़ार, दुबारा, चुनें, इसलिए over आवश्यक, प्रतीक्षा, पुनः, चयन करें, अतः.
- English nouns in Devanagari are normal (वीडियो, लिंक, लाइव). Keep brand names in Latin script.
- आप with देखें/देखिए for brands, often तुम for creators; तू only if the samples use it. Add जी after names and roles.
- End sentences with । (U+0964, never a pipe) or ".", per the samples, consistently; short captions may end with nothing or "!".
- Keep expected nukta (ज़रूर, ख़ास); one nasal spelling (जाएँ or जाएं) per post.

## Never assume
- First-person verbs mark the writer's gender: गया/गई, करता/करती हूँ, करूँगा/करूँगी. Follow the samples or stay neutral: मैंने … किया, मुझे बहुत अच्छा लगा, plural हम, or noun phrases.
- Add festival or religious greetings only if the brief or samples do.

## Formats
- Western digits; ₹ first, no space, lakh–crore grouping (₹1,25,000), "1.5 लाख", "2 करोड़"; dates d/M/yy or 16 सितंबर 2026.

## Avoid
- "Purifying" or Urdu-ising the author's words: vocabulary carries religious identity.
- Caste labels and loaded Kashmir place names.
- Zero-width joiners inside hashtags.
