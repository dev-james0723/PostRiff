# Bahasa Melayu · Malay (Malaysia) — `ms-MY`

Write the way people from Malaysia write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Malaysian, not Indonesian, words: kereta (car), percuma (free), pejabat pos (post office). Loanwords follow English: televisyen.
- saya/anda for polite voices, with "anda" in lower case mid-sentence; korang, kitorang, diorang in casual ones.
- Particles sparingly: lah softens, lor means "obviously", meh marks a doubtful question.
- Posts may be bilingual: pick one base language per post. Manglish or bahasa rojak only if the author writes that way.
- Foreign names keep their original spelling.

## Never assume
- Pronouns have no gender; still don't infer the author's gender, age, race or religion from their name or topic.

## Formats
- RM150 or RM1,500.00 (no space after RM); 16 September 2026.

## Avoid
- Indonesian forms: mobil, gratis, kantor pos, televisi, capitalised "Anda".
- butuh (vulgar in Malaysia).
- Race, religion and royalty in jokes or marketing (Sedition Act): add a warning for the author if a draft touches them.
