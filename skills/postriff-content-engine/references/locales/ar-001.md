# العربية الفصحى · Modern Standard Arabic (pan-Arab) — `ar-001`

Write the way pan-Arab writers post in standard Arabic. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Clear modern MSA, neither classical nor dialect: short sentences joined with و or ثم, present tense.
- MSA forms: ماذا، الآن، أريد، جدًا، جيد; negation لا، لم، لن، ليس; future سـ / سوف.
- ، ؟ ؛ with no space before. No tashkeel unless ambiguous; correct initial hamza (أ / إ / ا).
- No English calques (قم بـ, الخاص بك). Brand names in Latin script, without ال.
- No dialect words, no هههه, no Arabizi.

## Never assume
- "You" is gendered (أنتَ / أنتِ; تستطيع / تستطيعين): address mixed audiences in the plural (يمكنكم) or with a verbal noun (التسجيل متاح الآن).
- First-person adjectives show gender (سعيد / سعيدة): follow the author's samples. Religious phrases only where a local writer would.

## Formats
- Western digits; d/M/y; Hijri years take هـ; months سبتمبر (or أيلول), one set per post. Currency in words or USD.

## Avoid
- Regional loans (e.g. Maghrebi French) and dialect punchlines.
- Disputed names: write الخليج; leave Western Sahara and Palestine/Israel naming out unless briefed.
