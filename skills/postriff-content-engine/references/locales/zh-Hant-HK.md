# 繁體中文（香港）· Chinese — Traditional (Hong Kong) — `zh-Hant-HK`

Write the way people from Hong Kong write their own social posts in this variety. These are general conventions for the variety, not facts about the author: the author's VOICE.md decides tone and habits, and the approved facts decide content.

## Decisive markers
- Standard written Chinese (書面語) in Traditional characters: 是, 的, 了, 沒有. 嘅, 咗 or Cantonese particles turn it into Cantonese; only if the author's samples do.
- Quotes 「」 with 『』 inside; 《》 for books, albums and programmes; 〈〉 for articles or chapters.
- Full-width ，。、；：！？; —— and …… take two character widths.
- Hong Kong word shapes and orders: 質素 (service, people), 私隱, 擠擁, 取錄, 銜頭, 飯盒.
- Cantonese-sound transliterations: 梳化, 三文治, 荷里活.
- Keep English brand, place and personal English names as written.

## Tone, particles and address
- Use 嗎, 呢, 吧, 啊 sparingly; announcements often use none. Warmth comes from word choice (歡迎, 期待與你見面).
- Address readers as 你/你們, groups as 各位/大家. 您 reads stiff in a casual post.
- Keep it concise and understated. State achievements plainly (獲邀, 榮獲); stacked superlatives (最頂尖, 史上最強) read as bragging or 硬銷.
- Event posts: short hook, fact lines with emoji bullets (📍 🗓 ⏰), call to action (詳情請瀏覽). Hashtags at the end, Chinese and English mixed.

## Language mixing
- Insert single English words (「最Hit」) rather than whole English clauses; formal posts use fewer.
- Bilingual pages usually give the Chinese paragraph, then a separate English one.
- Spacing between Chinese and English varies; follow the author's samples.

## Never assume
- Don't guess anyone's gender: write 你, not 妳; use role nouns (讀者, 顧客) or 其 instead of 他/她 for generic people; use 先生/女士 only when gender is known.
- Don't invent the author's age, religion, relationship or district.

## Formats
- Dates: 2026年9月16日; short 16/9/2026 (day first); 16/9（週三）.
- Time: 下午3:00, 晚上7時30分 or 7:30pm.
- Numbers: 1,234.5; 萬/億 in prose.
- Currency: HK$ or $ before the number; 港元 in prose.

## Local rules to flag, not to enforce
- Under the National Security Law and the 2024 Safeguarding National Security Ordinance, don't add political slogans or protest references; if the author supplies them, add a warning for the author.
- Official usage calls the rest of the PRC 內地; don't present Hong Kong as a country.

## Avoid
- Taiwan forms (軟體, 網路, 計程車) and mainland forms (視頻, 信息, 質量 for service); readers may call the latter 支語.
- Simplified characters mixed in: it reads like cross-border marketing.
- Converting a Simplified or Taiwan draft.
- Hashtag walls, all-caps English, hard-sell lists.
