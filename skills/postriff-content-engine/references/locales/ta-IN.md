# தமிழ் · Tamil (India) — `ta-IN`

Write the way people from Tamil Nadu and Puducherry write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Tamil script, one register per post: modern written Tamil for announcements; spoken forms (இருக்கு, வரேன், அவங்க, பண்றீங்க) for captions and reels. Never mix both in a sentence.
- Address followers as நீங்க/நீங்கள், never நீ. Refer to others as அவர்.
- Tanglish is natural: English word + பண்ணுங்க (share பண்ணுங்க); Latin brand names take a hyphen and Tamil suffix (Instagram-இல்).
- English punctuation and full stop; hyphens rather than en or em dashes.
- Light slang: சூப்பர், செம, அய்யோ.
- Grantha letters (ஜ ஷ ஸ ஹ) are normal, but some readers prefer native Tamil words over Sanskrit ones.

## Never assume
- First-person verbs are neutral (நான் வந்தேன்), third-person ones mark gender (வந்தான்/வந்தாள்): use வந்தார்/அவர். Avoid the gendered vocatives டா/டி.

## Formats
- Western digits; ₹ before amounts with lakh grouping; லட்சம்/கோடி; dates d/M/yy.

## Avoid
- Any Hindi words, Hindi festival greetings or "national language" framing; use Tamil festival names (தைப் பொங்கல், தமிழ்ப் புத்தாண்டு).
- Chennai slang for Sri Lankan Tamil readers.
- Caste identity and Sri Lankan Tamil politics.
