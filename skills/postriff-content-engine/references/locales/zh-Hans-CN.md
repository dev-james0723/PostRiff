# 简体中文（中国）· Chinese — Simplified (China) — `zh-Hans-CN`

Write the way people from mainland China write their own social posts in this variety. These are general conventions for the variety, not facts about the author: the author's VOICE.md decides tone and habits, and the approved facts decide content.

## Decisive markers
- Simplified characters; Traditional reads as Hong Kong, Taiwan or a deliberate style.
- Quotes “” then ‘’; 《》 for every title (〈〉 only nested inside one); full-width punctuation; · between parts of foreign names.
- Mainland vocabulary: 视频, 软件, 网络, 信息, 质量, 出租车, 公交, 地铁, 点赞, 打印机.
- Casual particles 啊, 呀, 啦, 哦, 呢, 吧, 嘛; laughter 哈哈哈.
- Fit the platform: Xiaohongshu gets an emoji-rich title (often 【】), short paragraphs, emoji bullets, #tags at the end; Weibo uses #话题#; Douyin a short caption plus #tags.

## Tone, particles and address
- Xiaohongshu rewards a first-person "I tried it" tone with practical detail; obvious ads get penalised.
- Use 你 and drop pronouns where you can; 您 for customer service and older audiences. Address the audience as 大家; 家人们, 宝子们 are livestream styles.
- Slang is perishable: 集美 now often reads sarcastic; 绝绝子 and yyds read dated. Use slang only if the author does.
- Humble-bragging draws mockery; give concrete facts. Calls to action: 点赞收藏, 评论区聊聊.

## Language mixing
- Keep English brand names as written; otherwise use the natural Chinese term.
- Pinyin initialisms and Latin slang (yyds, NB) are informal; keep them out of professional posts unless the author uses them.

## Never assume
- 姐妹们 assumes a female audience; use 大家 unless the author targets women.
- Avoid 剩女, 娘炮, 女秘书 (write 秘书), 老外 (write 外国人), 乡下人 (write 农村人).
- Don't invent the author's age, religion, relationship or city.

## Formats
- Dates: 2026年9月16日; 2026/9/16; 9/16周三.
- Time: 19:30 or 晚上7点.
- Numbers: commas; 万/亿 (1.2万).
- Currency: ¥ or 元; 人民币 in prose; 块 colloquially.

## Local rules to flag, not to enforce
- Advertising law bans 国家级, 最高级 and 最佳-type terms, and platforms scan for similar absolute claims: add a warning for the author.
- Xiaohongshu penalises moving users off-platform (links, phone numbers, WeChat IDs): add a warning for the author.
- Keyword filtering is aggressive: add a warning for the author on political topics; no homophone evasions (河蟹).
- Never list Taiwan, Hong Kong or Macau as countries; use 国家和地区 in lists. Use 内地 relative to Hong Kong and Macau.

## Avoid
- Taiwan and Hong Kong forms (軟體, 網路, 的士, 影片 for online video) and 「」 quotes.
- Emoji and hashtag walls, 引流, dated memes as caricature.
