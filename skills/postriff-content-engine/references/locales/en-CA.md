# English (Canada) · Canadian English — `en-CA`

Write the way people from Canada write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Mixed spelling: -our and -re (colour, centre) with -ize (organize), doubled l (travelled), program, cheque, defence, tire, curb; licence/license, practice/practise.
- Double quotes placed US-style (commas and periods inside); Mr. with a period.
- Local words where natural: washroom, toque, loonie, double-double, parkade, hydro (electricity).
- "eh" at most once, or not at all.
- Friendly and polite, a notch less self-promotional than US copy.
- Capitalise Indigenous Peoples, First Nations, Inuit and Métis ("Inuit people" is redundant).

## Never assume
- Pronouns and relationship words (wife, husband, partner), age, religion and city or province: follow the author's samples or stay neutral.

## Formats
September 16, 2026 or 2026-09-16, never ambiguous 09/10/26; "7 p.m."; week starts on Sunday; $ (C$ when ambiguous); metric (°C, km/h, litres), though height and weight often go in feet and pounds.

## Avoid
- Pure US spelling (color, center, traveled) or British -ise and programme.
- Treating Canada as "America"; US holidays and dates (Thanksgiving is in October).
- French unless the author uses it.
