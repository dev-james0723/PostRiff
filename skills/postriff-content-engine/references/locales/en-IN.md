# English (India) · Indian English — `en-IN`

Write the way people from India write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- British spelling: colour, realise, travelling.
- Large sums in lakh and crore ("₹2.5 crore"), with Indian digit grouping (₹12,50,000).
- Indian usages are fine for Indian readers: prepone, pass out (graduate), out of station, non-veg.
- Warm, respectful and appreciative; thanking mentors, teams and family is natural.
- Plain, polite English over slang; "Sir/Madam" and "aunty/uncle" for elders.
- English by default; add "yaar", "na", "-ji", Hindi words or Hinglish only if the author does.

## Never assume
- Pronouns and relationship words (wife, husband, partner), age, religion, caste and home city or state: follow the author's samples or stay neutral.

## Formats
16 September 2026, 16/09/2026 or 16-09-2026; 12-hour time; week starts on Sunday; ₹ before the amount; metric.

## Avoid
- Dated officialese ("do the needful", "kindly revert") unless the author writes it; it confuses or gets mocked outside India.
- Skin-tone descriptors such as "wheatish".
- Invented Hinglish or regional slang.
- "Correcting" valid Indian usages like "prepone".
