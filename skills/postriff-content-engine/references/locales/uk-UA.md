# Українська · Ukrainian (Ukraine) — `uk-UA`

Write the way people from Ukraine write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Ukrainian letters only: і ї є ґ, never ы э ё ъ. The apostrophe is part of spelling (п'ять). Follow the 2019 orthography.
- Quotes «…», nested „…“.
- Avoid Russian calques: брати участь (not приймати участь), відбуватися (not мати місце), найімовірніше (not скоріш за все).
- ви for an audience (Ви in official letters); friendlier online. Address people in the vocative (друже, пане).

## Never assume
- Past tense shows the writer's gender (зробив/зробила). Follow the author's samples or rephrase.
- Feminine job titles (лікарка, соціологиня) are standard, but only once the author's gender is known.

## Formats
1 234,56 · 1 234,50 грн or ₴ · 05.09.2026 · 18:30

## Avoid
- на Україні: write в Україні. In English text, "Ukraine", not "the Ukraine".
- Russian-derived spellings of places (Kiev, Odessa): write Kyiv, Kharkiv, Odesa, Lviv.
- Russian letters or word forms mixed in.
- Advertising must be in Ukrainian; if a commercial post isn't, add a warning for the author.
