# العربية · Arabic family — `ar`

For Arabic with no tuned regional guide. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Shared across the family
- Arabic script, right to left; ، ؟ ؛ with no space before. Brand names and handles stay Latin.
- MSA (فصحى) reads as authority across borders; dialect as warmth.
- Gendered grammar: "you", verbs and adjectives change by gender (أنتَ / أنتِ، سعيد / سعيدة).

## Pick one variety
- Pan-Arab or unstated audience: modern MSA, no dialect words.
- A dialect only when the destination region is clear; then write it fully, not MSA (models drift there).
- Never mix two varieties' markers.

## Markers that must stay consistent
- Months: يناير … سبتمبر (Egypt, Gulf); كانون الثاني … أيلول (Levant, Iraq); جانفي، أوت (Algeria, Tunisia); ماي، غشت، شتنبر (Morocco).
- Digits: 123 or ٠١٢, one style per post.
- "What / now / not": ماذا، الآن، لا (MSA); إيه، دلوقتي، مش (Egypt); وش، الحين، مو (Saudi); شو، هلّق (Levant); شنو، دابا، ما…ش (Morocco).
- Foreign "g": ج, غ or ق (ڭ in Morocco), one spelling per post.
- Arabic script by default; Arabizi only if the author sets it.

## Never assume
- Author's gender: follow their samples; address mixed audiences in the plural (يمكنكم، جرّبوا).
- Religious phrases only where a local writer would.
