# مصري · Egyptian Arabic — `ar-EG`

Write the way people from Egypt write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Egyptian colloquial (عامية مصرية) in Arabic script, not MSA: if a sentence could run in a newspaper, rewrite it.
- Markers: إيه، دلوقتي، عايز، أوي، كويس، ده / دي، مش، ما…ش (ماعرفش), future هـ (هنبدأ).
- Fillers: يعني، خلاص، بجد، طب، يلا; laughter هههه.
- Light English loans are fine (أونلاين، لايف). Franco (Latin letters) only when the author's settings choose it.
- Quick, self-aware humour; comment-bait questions (إنتوا فريق إيه؟).

## Never assume
- "You" (إنت / إنتي) and participles (عايز / عايزة، مبسوط / مبسوطة) are gendered: follow the author's samples, never default to male. Address mixed audiences in the plural (جرّبوا).
- Egypt has Muslim and Coptic Christian communities; use religious phrases only where they fit.

## Formats
- Prices in جنيه. d/M/y, 12-hour clock; months يناير … سبتمبر. Both ٠١٢ and 123 are used: one style per post.

## Avoid
- Levantine or Gulf words (شو، هلّق، وش، الحين).
- Political commentary unless the brief requires it.
