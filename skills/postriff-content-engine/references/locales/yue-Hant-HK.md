# 廣東話（香港）· Cantonese (Hong Kong) — `yue-Hant-HK`

Write the way people from Hong Kong write their own social posts in Cantonese. These are general conventions for the variety, not facts about the author: the author's VOICE.md decides tone and habits, and the approved facts decide content.

## Decisive markers
- Traditional characters with Cantonese grammar words: 係, 唔, 冇, 佢, 嘅, 咗, 緊, 啲, 咁, 哋, 喺, 嘢, 睇, 嚟, 而家, 點解. Never put 的, 了, 是 or 什麼 into a Cantonese clause.
- Cantonese word order: 佢高過我, 你行先, 俾本書我.
- Sentence-final particles, about one per sentence or clause: 啦 (suggestion), 喇 (new state), 㗎 (explaining), 喎 (noticing), 咋 (only), 囉, 咩, 啫. None reads as 書面語; one on every line reads childish.
- Standard spellings 嘅, 咗, 㗎, 嘢, 嚟, 啱, not 既, 左, 架, 野, 黎, 岩, unless the author's samples use them.
- Casual posts often drop the full stop and break lines, ending with emoji, ！！ or …. Quotes 「」, titles 《》.

## Tone, particles and address
- Posts run from written notice, to a written body with Cantonese at warm moments (感謝大家嘅支持！), to full Cantonese with English words. Match the author's samples.
- A typical post opens in Cantonese, gives date, time, place and price as plain lines, and closes with a Cantonese call to action (想嚟嘅快啲留位啦, 有興趣可以 PM 我).
- Use 你/你哋, 大家, 各位. 您 sounds out of place in Cantonese.
- Teasing, self-deprecating and pun humour is common. Showing off reads as 曬, hard selling as 硬銷; prefer a friendly nudge.
- Instagram and Threads: short lines, moderate emoji, English or bilingual hashtags at the end.

## Language mixing
- Insert single English words that take Cantonese grammar (check 咗, book 位, send 俾你); don't alternate whole English sentences.
- Established loans stay in characters: 的士, 士多, 三文治, 朱古力, 貼士.
- Non-native blend signs: 的 and 嘅 in one clause, switching 是/係, 睇了 for 睇咗.

## Never assume
- 佢/佢哋 is gender-neutral; use it. Don't use 妳, 靚仔 or 靚女 to guess anyone's gender.
- Don't invent the author's age, religion, relationship or district.

## Formats
- Dates: day first (16/9) or 9月16日; 星期六 / 禮拜六.
- Time: 7點半, 下晝3點, 夜晚8點.
- Money: $300 or HK$300; 300蚊 in casual lines.

## Local rules to flag, not to enforce
- As for Hong Kong written Chinese: don't add political slogans or protest references; if the author supplies them, add a warning for the author.

## Avoid
- Vulgar Cantonese, stand-ins (7 for 柒, D for 啲) and LIHKG in-group slang.
- Mandarin words, or Taiwan and mainland vocabulary, inside Cantonese.
- Converting a Mandarin draft; no converter produces Cantonese.
- Piled-up slang or particles as caricature; hard-sell superlative lists.
