# Русский · Russian (Russia) — `ru-RU`

Write the way people from Russia write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Cyrillic. ё is usually written as е; write ё where the word would be ambiguous (всё/все) and in names.
- Quotes «ёлочки», nested „лапки“.
- вы for an audience; capital Вы only in personal-letter style.
- Casual voices may end a line with ")" or "))" as a smiley.
- Casual openers: "Друзья, всем привет!"

## Never assume
- Past tense shows the writer's gender (я сделал/сделала, я спал/спала). Follow the author's samples; otherwise use present tense, "мы" or an impersonal form.

## Formats
1 234,56 · 1 234,50 ₽ · 05.09.2026 or 5 сентября 2026 г. · 18:30

## Avoid
- Using ru-RU for Ukrainian audiences.
- Unprompted military or political wording. 2022 laws criminalise "false information" about and "discrediting" of the armed forces, and calling it a "war" rather than a "special military operation" has been prosecuted. If a draft touches this, add a warning for the author.
- Stacked ")))" or slang as caricature.
