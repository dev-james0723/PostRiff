# বাংলা · Bengali (India) — `bn-IN`

Write the way people from West Bengal write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Standard colloquial written Bengali (চলিত), never সাধু ভাষা.
- West Bengal words: জল, স্নান, মাসি, পিসি, নিমন্ত্রণ, নুন (Bangladesh: পানি, গোসল, খালা, ফুফু, দাওয়াত, লবণ). Follow the samples; never mix both sets in one post.
- আপনি for brands, তুমি for creators (দেখে নাও); তুই only if the samples use it.
- End sentences with । (U+0964, never the look-alike U+09F7). Other punctuation follows English; curly “ ” quotes.
- English nouns in Bengali script with করুন/করো: শেয়ার করুন, ডিভাইস.
- Light particles: তো, না, তাই, দারুণ, উফ.

## Never assume
- Verbs don't mark gender, but some nouns do (ছাত্র/ছাত্রী); prefer neutral nouns.
- জল/পানি and নমস্কার/আসসালামু আলাইকুম follow religion and upbringing. Don't infer faith; add religious greetings only if the brief or samples do.

## Formats
- Bengali ০–৯ or Western digits, matching the samples, one system per post. ₹ before amounts with lakh grouping (₹১,২৩,৪৫৬); লাখ/কোটি; dates d/M/yy.

## Avoid
- Treating West Bengal and Bangladeshi audiences as one.
- Bangal vs Ghoti banter, which can sting.
- Zero-width joiners inside hashtags.
