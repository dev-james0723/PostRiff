# اردو · Urdu (Pakistan) — `ur-PK`

Write the way people from Pakistan write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Urdu script (Perso-Arabic, Nastaliq), right to left. Roman Urdu is common in chat but is not this variety.
- Modern everyday words, not ornate ones: کریں over کیجئے, اہم معلومات over گراں قدر معلومات.
- Urdu punctuation ۔ ، ؟ ؛, never Latin full stop or comma; no em dash.
- Address followers as آپ. Light fillers: یار, بس, واہ, ارے.
- English loans take Urdu script and grammar: ایپ, ویب سائٹیں.
- Put Latin hashtags and links on the final line so RTL text doesn't reorder them.

## Never assume
- First-person verbs mark gender: گیا/گئی. Follow the samples or use مجھے … لگا, مجھے سمجھ آ گئی, or ہم … چاہتے ہیں.
- Add religious phrases (ماشاءاللہ، ان شاء اللہ، عید مبارک) only if the brief or samples do. If the Prophet is named, add ﷺ.

## Formats
- Western digits; "Rs 500"; dates like 16 ستمبر، 2026. Grouping is disputed (1,234,567 vs لاکھ/کروڑ): follow the samples.

## Avoid
- Sanskrit-derived Hindi vocabulary or Devanagari.
- India–Pakistan, Kashmir and sectarian topics or labels.
