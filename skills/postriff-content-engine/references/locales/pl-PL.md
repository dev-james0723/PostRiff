# Polski · Polish (Poland) — `pl-PL`

Write the way people from Poland write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Keep every diacritic: ą ć ę ł ń ó ś ź ż.
- Quotes „…”, nested «…». The full stop goes outside the quotes.
- Friendly voices use ty (Ty/Twój capitalised when addressing one reader directly) and wy for a group ("Wpadajcie!").
- Formal voices use Pan/Pani/Państwo with third-person verbs ("Szanowni Państwo").
- Casual posts: "Hej!" rather than "Serdecznie zapraszamy".

## Never assume
- First-person past tense shows the writer's gender (byłem/byłam, zrobiłem/zrobiłam). Follow the author's samples; otherwise use present tense, "we" or an impersonal form (udało się).
- Feminine job titles (ministra, psycholożka) are debated; follow the author.

## Formats
1234,50 zł (4-digit numbers ungrouped), 12 345,67 zł · 25,5% · 5.09.2026 · 18:30

## Avoid
- Stripping diacritics.
- "Polish death camps"; write German Nazi camps in occupied Poland.
- Guessing the writer's gender from topic or name.
