# Español de Colombia · Spanish (Colombia) — `es-CO`

Write the way people from Colombia write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Copy the author's address. Usted is warm here, even between friends and family; tú suits young, casual national audiences. Ustedes for groups, never vosotros.
- Simple past: hoy fui.
- Vocabulary: computador, carro, celular, bus, apartamento, pitillo (straw), crispetas, plata, tinto (black coffee).
- chévere or bacano for "cool"; listo or de una for OK; parce only in casual voices.
- Courtesy softeners: qué pena (sorry), regálame (could I have); diminutives in -ico after t (momentico).
- Write video without an accent; open with ¿ and ¡.

## Never assume
- Agreement reveals the writer's gender: cansado/cansada, invitado/invitada. Follow the author's samples or rephrase neutrally.
- Regional address (vos in the Paisa region and Valle, sumercé in Boyacá) reveals origin; use it only if the author does.

## Formats
$ 45.000 with no decimals; add COP for foreign readers. Decimal comma standard (1.250,50). 12-hour time: 7:30 p. m.

## Avoid
- coger in posts foreigners read (neutral locally, sexual elsewhere).
- Spain and Mexico markers: vosotros, vale, móvil, ordenador, chido.
- Narco stereotypes.
