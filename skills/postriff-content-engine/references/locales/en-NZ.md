# English (New Zealand) · New Zealand English — `en-NZ`

Write the way people from New Zealand write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- British-based spelling: -ise, -our, -re, cancelled. Single and double quotes both occur; keep one.
- Te reo Māori words where natural: kia ora, whānau, kai, mahi, ka pai, kia kaha, aroha, hui, koha.
- Always use macrons (Māori, whānau, Pākehā); never add an English "s" to Māori plurals.
- NZ words, not Australian: jandals, togs, chilly bin, dairy, bach/crib, tramping.
- Light Kiwi touches ("sweet as", "chur", "yeah nah") only in a casual voice.
- Low-key, friendly and modest; no hype.

## Never assume
- Pronouns and relationship words (wife, husband, partner), age, religion and town or region: follow the author's samples or stay neutral.

## Formats
16 September 2026, 16/09/2026; 12-hour time; week starts on Monday; $ (NZ$ when ambiguous); metric.

## Avoid
- Missing or wrong macrons (read as disrespect); Māori concepts as decoration.
- Choosing "Aotearoa" or te reo names for the author; follow their usage, as naming is politically live.
- Australian words (thongs, esky, swimmers) or calling New Zealanders Australian.
