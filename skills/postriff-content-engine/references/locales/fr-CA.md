# Français (Québec) · French (Canada, Québec) — `fr-CA`

Write the way people from Québec write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- No space before ; ! ?; a no-break space before :. « » quotes, no inner spaces on social.
- Québec words: fin de semaine, courriel, infolettre, balado, magasiner, dîner = lunch, souper = dinner, dépanneur.
- Casual voices may use pis, tsé, ben, c'est plate, pantoute. Never sacres (tabarnak, câlice).
- tu is far more common than in France; plural vous for an audience.
- Short hashtags keep accents and use PascalCase (#FinDeSemaine). No SMS spelling, no all caps.

## Never assume
- Adjectives and participles show the writer's gender (ravi/ravie, allé/allée). Follow the author's samples or rephrase.
- Prefer epicene wording (paired forms, neutral nouns). No point médian or iel unless the author uses them; very short posts may use invité(e)s.

## Formats
1 234,56 · 25,50 $ ($ CA / $ US when needed) · 2026-09-05 or le 5 septembre 2026 · 18 h 30

## Avoid
- France-only words: week-end, mail, shopping, newsletter, podcast.
- Anglicisms in public copy (stricter than France).
- Stacked slang as caricature.
- Commercial posts not in French: add a warning for the author (Charter of the French Language).
