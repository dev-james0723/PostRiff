# 繁體中文（台灣）· Chinese — Traditional (Taiwan) — `zh-Hant-TW`

Write the way people from Taiwan write their own social posts in this variety. These are general conventions for the variety, not facts about the author: the author's VOICE.md decides tone and habits, and the approved facts decide content.

## Decisive markers
- Traditional characters in Taiwan's standard shapes: 著, 裡, 峰, 麵, 群. Write 台 (台灣, 台北) in social posts unless the author writes 臺.
- Quotes 「」 then 『』, with sentence-final punctuation inside; 《》 for books, albums and films; 〈〉 for articles, chapters and single songs.
- Taiwan vocabulary: 軟體, 網路, 影片, 資訊, 品質, 計程車, 捷運, 按讚, 印表機.
- Light particles where natural: 喔 (friendly reminder), 欸, 啦, 耶, 吧. Laughter: 哈哈哈, XD, 笑死. The wave dash ～ is common in casual lines.
- A light Taiwanese Hokkien touch (歹勢, 夯) or loans like 便當, 達人 reads local; heavy Hokkien from a non-Hokkien voice reads forced.

## Tone, particles and address
- Warm, polite and chatty; self-deprecation and cute softening work. Don't stack particles.
- Use 你 in posts for warmth; 您 for singular "you" in formal or service copy; 你們 (not 您們) for plural; 大家, 各位 for groups. Brand pages often speak as 小編.
- Sponsored-sounding superlatives (史上最強, 必買) read as 業配; prefer first-hand detail.
- Threads: conversational first person, few or no hashtags. Instagram: moderate emoji, place hashtags (#台北). Dcard and PTT titles often carry a bracketed tag ([心得], [情報]).
- Cute spellings (捏, 餒) suit playful posts only.

## Language mixing
- English brand names and short English words are common.
- Spacing between Chinese and Latin letters or digits varies; follow the author's samples.

## Never assume
- 妳 is widely used for a female "you"; write 你 unless the author's samples show otherwise.
- Prefer 各位, 大家 or 同仁 over gendered group address.
- Don't invent the author's age, religion, relationship or city.

## Formats
- Dates: 2026/9/16; 9/16（週三）; 2026年9月16日. ROC years (民國115年 = 2026) only in government-style contexts or if the author uses them.
- Time: 下午3:00, 晚上7:30, 凌晨.
- Numbers: commas; 萬/億 (1.2萬).
- Currency: NT$ or 元 (500元); 塊 colloquially.

## Avoid
- Mainland vocabulary (視頻, 質量, 信息, 網絡, 優化): it reads foreign and draws 支語 criticism. Also Hong Kong forms (的士, 軟件) and Simplified characters.
- 注音文 (ㄉ for 的, ㄏㄏ): many find it hard to read.
- Dated terms: write 移工 (not 外勞), 中國籍女性 (not 大陸妹), 封鎖清單/允許清單 (not 黑名單/白名單).
- Write 台灣, never 中國台灣 or 台灣省. Naming the PRC (中國 vs 中國大陸) signals politics; follow the author or leave it out.
- Converting a Simplified draft; write natively. No 加LINE invitations or emoji walls.
