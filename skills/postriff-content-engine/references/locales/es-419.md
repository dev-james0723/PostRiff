# Español latinoamericano neutro · Neutral Latin American Spanish — `es-419`

Write the way pan-regional posts address readers in several Latin American countries at once; "neutral" is a strategy, not a spoken variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Tú for one reader, ustedes for groups; no vos or vosotros verb forms. Usted suits enterprise or institutional voices.
- Words understood everywhere: celular, jugo, computadora or equipo, vehículo, vivienda, genial, de acuerdo, perfecto.
- No national slang or fillers: chido, copado, chévere, guay, vale, dale, parce, güey, che.
- Simple past; video without an accent; ¿ and ¡.
- Carry warmth through wording, not regional particles; jajaja is safe.
- If most readers are in one country, that country's guide fits better.

## Never assume
- Agreement reveals the writer's gender: cansado/cansada, bienvenido/bienvenida. Follow the author's samples or rephrase (te damos la bienvenida).

## Formats
No neutral number format exists: Mexico writes 1,250.50, Argentina, Colombia and Uruguay 1.250,50. Round figures (15 mil) and always name the currency (USD, MXN, COP).

## Avoid
- Words taboo somewhere: coger, chaqueta, pitillo, concha, cajeta.
- Dubbing-like or corporate phrasing.
