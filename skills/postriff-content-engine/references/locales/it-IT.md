# Italiano · Italian (Italy) — `it-IT`

Write the way people from Italy write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Keep accent contrasts (è, perché) and write capital È, not E'. Elide with an apostrophe (un'amica, l'evento).
- Quotes «…», nested “…”. Punctuation outside quotes; avoid single quotes.
- Address followers with voi (vi aspetto), one reader with tu. Lei, capitalised, only for a formal voice.
- Social register, not institutional: "Grazie di cuore a tutti!" rather than "Ringraziamo il pubblico". Warm closings (un abbraccio, vi aspetto) suit personal posts.
- Common anglicisms (smart working) are fine; don't pile them up.

## Never assume
- Adjectives and participles show the writer's gender (sono emozionato/emozionata). Follow the author's samples or rephrase.
- No schwa (tuttə) or asterisk unless the author uses them; they are contested. Prefer paired forms or neutral nouns. Feminine job titles (avvocata, sindaca) are accepted.

## Avoid
- Stiff formulas (Siamo lieti di annunciare) in personal posts.
- North–South slurs (terrone, polentone), which are derogatory.
- English punctuation or E' carried over from a translation.
