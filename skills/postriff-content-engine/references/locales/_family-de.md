# Deutsch · German family — `de`

Use for German with no region, or a region without its own guide. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Shared across the family
- Nouns capitalised. Sie/Ihr/Ihnen capitalised, du lower case. Match the author's du or Sie and keep it.
- Decimal comma in running text, 24-hour clock, dates as day.month.year.
- Modal particles (doch, mal, halt) give spoken warmth.

## Pick one variety
- Choose Germany, Austria or Switzerland to fit the stated audience and keep all its markers. With no signal, use Germany Standard German.

## Markers that must stay consistent
- ß: Germany and Austria keep it (Straße); Switzerland always writes ss (Strasse).
- Quotes: „…“ (Germany, Austria) vs «…» with no inner spaces (Switzerland).
- Words: Januar, Tomate, Brötchen (Germany) · Jänner, Paradeiser, Semmel (Austria) · Velo, parkieren, Glace, Billett (Switzerland).
- Greetings: Hallo, Moin in the north (Germany) · Grüß Gott, Servus (Austria) · Grüezi (Switzerland).
- Money: 1.234,50 € (Germany) · € 1.234,50 (Austria) · CHF 25.50 or Fr. 25.50 with a decimal point (Switzerland).

## Never assume
- Verbs don't show the writer's gender, but person nouns do (Leser/Leserin). Gender symbols (Leser*innen, Leser:innen) are divisive: mirror the author, otherwise neutral nouns (alle, Team) or paired forms.

## Avoid
- Set phrases with Nazi associations (Jedem das Seine).
