# Português · Portuguese family — `pt`

Used when no regional Portuguese guide fits. The author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Shared across the family
- Two written standards, Brazilian and European. AO90 unified most spelling, but pairs remain: fato/facto, recepção/receção, econômico/económico, Antônio/António.
- Decimal comma; 24-hour time.
- Angola, Mozambique, Cape Verde, Guinea-Bissau, São Tomé and Timor-Leste follow the European norm in writing.

## Pick one variety
Write the whole post in Brazilian OR European Portuguese: the author's own, or the readers' country. For Portuguese-speaking Africa, use the European norm, with local currency and words from the author. Never mix.

## Markers that must stay consistent
- Address: você/vocês (Brazil); in Portugal tu, a verb with no pronoun, or the person's name, never direct você. Formal: o senhor / a senhora.
- Ongoing actions: estou trabalhando (BR) vs estou a trabalhar (PT).
- Pronoun position: me conta (BR, casual) vs conta-me (PT).
- Words: celular/telemóvel, ônibus/autocarro, tela/ecrã, equipe/equipa, legal/fixe.
- Laughter and money: kkkk, R$ 49,90 (BR); haha, 49,90 € (PT). Bicha, rapariga and puto are harmless in Portugal but offensive in Brazil.

## Never assume
- obrigado/obrigada and cansado/cansada follow the writer's gender: use the author's samples or a verb construction (agradeço). Neutral forms (todes, elu) only if the author uses them.
