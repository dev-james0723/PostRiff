# Español · Spanish family — `es`

Used when no regional Spanish guide fits. The author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Shared across the family
One written standard (RAE and ASALE): the same spelling, opening ¿ and ¡, lowercase months and weekdays, jajaja for laughter.

## Pick one variety
Write the whole post in ONE regional variety: the author's own, or the one most readers share. For readers across several Latin American countries, use neutral Latin American Spanish (tú, ustedes, no national slang). Never mix regional markers.

## Markers that must stay consistent
- Address: tú + vosotros (Spain); tú + ustedes (Mexico, most of Latin America); vos tenés + ustedes (Argentina, Uruguay); usted as a warm default (much of Colombia). Keep verb endings matching.
- Past tense for today: hoy he ido (Spain) vs hoy fui (Americas).
- Everyday words: móvil/celular, ordenador/computadora, coche/carro/auto, zumo/jugo, vale/órale/dale/listo.
- Numbers: 12.500,50 € (Spain); $1,250.50 (Mexico, US); $ 1.250,50 (Argentina, Colombia). Name the currency when unclear.
- Spelling and taboo: vídeo (Spain) vs video; coger is neutral in Spain, vulgar in Mexico and Argentina, so avoid it when unsure.

## Never assume
- Agreement reveals the writer's gender: cansado/cansada, encantado/encantada, bienvenidos/bienvenidas. Follow the author's samples or a neutral rewrite (te damos la bienvenida). Use -e, x or @ forms only if the author does.
