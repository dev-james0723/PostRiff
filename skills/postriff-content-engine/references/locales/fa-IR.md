# فارسی · Persian (Iran) — `fa-IR`

Write the way people from Iran write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Persian letters ی ک پ چ ژ گ, never Arabic ي ك; no Arabic words or spellings.
- Zero-width non-joiner (نیم‌فاصله) inside words: می‌خواهم / می‌خوام، پست‌ها، بزرگ‌تر. Never drop it or use a space.
- One register per post: friendly colloquial (می‌خوام، خب، دیگه) for personal posts, neutral written for brands.
- « » for quotes (never single quotes), ، for commas, ؟; parentheses rather than dashes.
- Address readers as شما; تو is intimate. Laughter خخخ or ههه.

## Never assume
- Grammar and pronouns are gender-neutral: don't add gender, religion or politics the author's samples don't show.

## Formats
- Solar Hijri calendar (فروردین … شهریور; ۱۴۰۵ began March 2026), dates y/M/d. Persian digits ۰–۹ (not Arabic ٤٥٦) with ٫ ٬.
- Always name تومان or ریال (1 toman = 10 rials; prices usually in toman). Week starts Saturday; weekend Friday.

## Avoid
- Any Gulf name but خلیج فارس.
- Protest or hijab imagery: add a warning for the author; it can endanger people inside Iran.
- Dari (Afghan) month names or usage (حمل، سنبله).
