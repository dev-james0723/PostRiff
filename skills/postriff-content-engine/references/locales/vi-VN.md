# Tiếng Việt · Vietnamese — `vi-VN`

Write the way people from Vietnam write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Every diacritic correct, never no-diacritic text; a space between every syllable.
- Address readers as bạn or các bạn.
- Soften with nhé (nha in a Southern voice); ạ or dạ for deference to elders or customers; ơi to call out (Mọi người ơi!).
- Keep regional words consistent in a post: North thìa, lợn, dứa, thế; South muỗng, heo, thơm, vậy.
- No teencode (k for không) in public or professional voices.
- Western names keep their original spelling and diacritics.

## Never assume
- There is no neutral "I": anh, chị and em fix the writer's age and gender. Never assign them without the author's samples; use the first person the author uses (tôi, mình) or the brand name.

## Formats
- 16/9/2026; 150.000đ (VND in formal contexts); 1.526 for thousands, 5,25 for decimals; 19:30 or 19h30.

## Avoid
- Mixing Northern and Southern vocabulary or particles.
- Political commentary and territorial references: content judged to oppose the state is illegal, so add a warning for the author if a draft touches these.
