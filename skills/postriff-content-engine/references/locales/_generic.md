# Generic locale guide · Any language without a researched guide

Use this when the destination's language has no locale guide and no family guide. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Write natively
- Write the post natively in the destination language. Never translate word for word from another regional variant or from the language the brief is written in.
- Use the script, punctuation and quotation marks standard for the locale, and its usual number, date, time and currency formats.
- If the destination names a region, keep to that region's variety throughout; don't mix in markers from sibling varieties.
- If unsure about a regional word, choose the widely understood standard form.
- Match the register of the author's samples; don't drift into a stiff, translated tone.

## Names and hashtags
- Keep proper nouns as the author wrote them, unless the locale has an established form for that name.
- Keep hashtags in the locale's own language unless the author uses English ones.

## Never assume
- Never assume the author's gender, age or religion. Where grammar would reveal them (gendered verbs, adjectives or pronouns, honorifics, self-reference), use a neutral construction or follow the author's samples.
- Don't add religious greetings, family details or location details that the approved facts don't contain.

## Avoid
- Slang, dialect spellings or regional jokes the author doesn't use.
- Making a claim stronger than the source.
