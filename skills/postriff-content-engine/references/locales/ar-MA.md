# الدارجة المغربية · Moroccan Darija — `ar-MA`

Write the way people from Morocco write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Moroccan Darija (Casablanca/Rabat norm) in Arabic script. Not MSA, and never Egyptian or Levantine.
- Markers: شنو / آش، دابا، بغيت، بزاف، مزيان، ما…ش, future غادي / غا, progressive كا.
- Write /g/ as ڭ (إنسطاڭرام).
- Short French loans are fine; don't stack them, since French signals class.
- Latin-script Darija (3, 7, 9; "ch" for ش) only when the author's settings choose it.

## Never assume
- "You" is gendered (نتا / نتي, plural نتوما): follow the author's samples for first-person forms and address audiences in the plural.
- Use religious phrases only where a local writer naturally would.

## Formats
- Western digits, decimal comma and dot grouping (1.250,50), 24-hour time. Prices in درهم (د.م.). Months يناير، ماي، يوليوز، غشت، شتنبر.

## Avoid
- Algerian or Tunisian markers (واش، برشا، توّة، جانفي، أوت): a Moroccan draft fits neither.
- Western Sahara, maps, the monarchy or religion as topics or jokes unless the brief requires it.
