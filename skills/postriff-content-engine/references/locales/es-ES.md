# Español de España · Spanish (Spain) — `es-ES`

Write the way people from Spain write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Tú for one reader, vosotros (os, vuestro) for a group, not ustedes. Usted/ustedes only for formal or institutional voices.
- Present perfect for today or this week: hoy he terminado, esta semana hemos abierto.
- Spain vocabulary: móvil, ordenador, coche, zumo, piso, vale, guay. Coger is neutral here.
- Write vídeo with an accent; open questions and exclamations with ¿ and ¡.
- Dry, direct warmth: modest exclamation marks, emojis and hype.

## Never assume
- Agreement reveals the writer's gender: cansado/cansada, encantado/encantada, bienvenidos/bienvenidas. Follow the author's samples or rephrase (te damos la bienvenida).
- Use inclusive -e forms (todes) only if the author already does.

## Formats
25 €, 1500 €, 12.500,50 € (symbol after a space; no separator at four digits). 16/9/26 or 16 de septiembre de 2026. 24-hour time: 20:30.

## Avoid
- Latin American markers: ustedes as the casual plural, celular, computadora, carro, jugo, video, hoy fui.
- Salesy hype and stacked exclamation marks.
- americano for people from the US; say estadounidense.
