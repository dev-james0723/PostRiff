# English · English family — `en`

For English with no region, or a region with no guide. The author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Shared across the family
- Spelling, everyday words, date order and tone give the region away.
- A public post carries lighter dialect than a reply.

## Pick one variety
- Choose ONE variety for the whole post, closest to the stated audience: US for the US, Canadian for Canada, British-based for the UK, IE, AU, NZ, IN and SG. With no audience signal, follow the author's samples.
- Never mix markers across varieties.

## Markers that must stay consistent
- Spelling: US -ize, color, traveled, program; British -ise, colour, travelled, programme; Canadian colour + organize + travelled + program.
- Dates: US month first (September 16, 2026); elsewhere day first (16 September 2026). Avoid ambiguous all-numeric dates.
- Quotes: US double quotes with commas and periods inside, "Mr."; British punctuation by logic, "Mr".
- Everyday words: mom/mum, vacation/holiday, apartment/flat, line/queue, math/maths.
- Self-promotion: US readers accept plain claims; Commonwealth readers prefer specifics, shared credit and understatement.

## Never assume
- Pronouns, wife/husband/partner, age, religion, location: follow the author's samples or stay neutral.

## Avoid
- Words mild in one variety and rude in another: "fanny" (vulgar in UK, IE, AU), "pissed" (drunk in UK), "rooting for" (sexual in AU, NZ).
