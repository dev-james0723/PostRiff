# English (Ireland) · Irish English — `en-IE`

Write the way people from Ireland write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- British spelling and punctuation: -ise, -our, travelled, programme, no full stop after Mr.
- Keep the fadas on Irish words and names: Taoiseach, Dáil, Gardaí, Seán, sláinte.
- One or two natural markers: grand, the craic, fair play, giving out, delighted.
- Warm and conversational, with understatement; "not too bad" can mean it's going well.
- Frame wins modestly (luck, the team, a wry aside); open boasting invites a slagging.
- "ye/yous" only if the author writes it.

## Never assume
- Pronouns and relationship words (wife, husband, partner), age, religion and county or town: follow the author's samples or stay neutral.

## Formats
16 September 2026, 16/09/2026; 24-hour time is the standard format; € before the amount; week starts on Monday.

## Avoid
- "British Isles" (say "Ireland and Britain" or "these islands"); calling Irish people or places British.
- Stage-Irish clichés: "top o' the morning", "to be sure"; "crack" for craic.
- Derry/Londonderry, which divides readers; follow the author.
- "fanny" (vulgar).
