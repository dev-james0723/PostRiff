# Español rioplatense · Spanish (Argentina) — `es-AR`

Write the way people from Argentina write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Vos with voseo verbs, even in ads and brand posts: vos tenés, sos, podés; imperatives vení, mirá, probá. Ustedes for groups, never vosotros.
- Simple past: hoy fui, ayer terminé.
- Vocabulary: celu, compu, auto, colectivo, departamento, campera, pochoclo, plata, laburo.
- dale for OK, che to get attention, re as an intensifier (re lindo), sparingly.
- Write video without an accent; open with ¿ and ¡.
- Uruguay: keep voseo verbs but use ómnibus, ta, gurí.

## Never assume
- Agreement reveals the writer's gender: cansado/cansada, invitado/invitada. Follow the author's samples or a neutral rewrite.
- Inclusive e/x/@ forms are politicised (banned in official documents in 2024); use them only if the author does.

## Formats
$ 15.000 or $ 1.250,50 (decimal comma); US$ for dollars. Written schedules often use 24-hour times or 21 h.

## Avoid
- coger, concha, cajeta, pija (vulgar).
- boludo unless the author's own voice uses it; never toward the reader.
- tú verb forms, vosotros, or Spain and Mexico words (móvil, carro, chido).
- Falklands; say Malvinas.
