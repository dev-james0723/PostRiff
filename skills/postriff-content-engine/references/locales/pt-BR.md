# Português do Brasil · Portuguese (Brazil) — `pt-BR`

Write the way people from Brazil write their own social posts in this variety. These are general conventions for the variety, not facts about the author: the author's VOICE.md decides tone and habits, and the approved facts decide content.

## Decisive markers
- Address readers as você/vocês; a gente for "we" suits casual posts.
- Casual writing puts pronouns before the verb: me conta, se inscreve.
- Gerund for ongoing actions: estou trabalhando.
- Brazilian spelling: econômico, Antônio, fato (fact), recepção, registrar, ideia.
- Curly quotes “ ”; decimal comma and point thousands (1.500,50).
- Often no article before possessives: meu livro.

## Tone, particles and address
- Warm and emotive: diminutives (cafezinho, rapidinho), emoji-rich captions, direct appeals to gente.
- Fillers: né?, tipo, beleza, valeu, tá bom; grana for money in casual voices.
- Laughter: kkkk for casual, close audiences; haha(ha) for neutral or professional posts; rsrs reads older and can sound forced.
- Authors and brands both use você; o senhor / a senhora only for formal one-to-one address.
- Tu is regional (the South, parts of the North and North-east), often as tu vai; only if the author uses it.
- Self-promotion on LinkedIn and Instagram usually works framed as gratitude or learning.

## Language mixing
- English marketing words are common (feedback, insights, live, post); match the author's level.

## Never assume
- Agreement reveals the writer's gender: obrigado (man) / obrigada (woman), cansado/cansada, animado/animada. Follow the author's samples or use a verb construction (agradeço, fiquei feliz).
- Neutral forms (todes, elu) are politically charged; use only if the author does.
- Don't assume the author's age, religion, state or relationship.

## Formats
- Money: R$ 49,90 (symbol, non-breaking space, comma decimals).
- Dates: 16/09/2026.
- Time: 24-hour, 19:30; 19h or 19h30 is common in event posts.

## Local rules to flag, not to enforce
- Public-body authors: add a warning for the author that Law 15.263/2025 tells public administration not to use new gender forms (todes, elu).

## Avoid
- European markers: estar a + infinitive, conta-me, telemóvel, ecrã, equipa, facto, económico.
- Words harmless in Portugal but loaded here: rapariga (pejorative regional sense), bicha (slur), puto (angry or vulgar), propina (bribe).
- Overdoing kkkk, diminutives or slang as caricature.
