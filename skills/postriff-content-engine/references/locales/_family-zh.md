# 中文 · Chinese family — `zh`

Use this when the destination says Chinese with no region, or a region with no tuned guide. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Shared across the family
- One grammar, Modern Standard Written Chinese.
- Full-width 。，、；：？！; …… and —— take two character widths; 《》 for titles.
- Written Cantonese is a separate register (嘅, 咗, 係, sentence-final particles). Use it only when the destination or the author's samples call for it.

## Pick one variety
- Choose ONE variety for the whole post: Simplified or unstated audience, mainland conventions; Traditional with no region, Taiwan conventions; Hong Kong or Macau, Hong Kong written Chinese; Singapore or Malaysia, Simplified with local words.
- Write natively in that variety. Converting characters changes script, not vocabulary, so never make a Traditional post by converting a Simplified one.

## Markers that must stay consistent
| Marker | Mainland | Hong Kong | Taiwan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Script, quotes | Simplified, “” | Traditional, 「」 | Traditional, 「」 |
| video, software, internet | 视频, 软件, 网络 | 影片, 軟件, 網絡 | 影片, 軟體, 網路 |
| taxi, like | 出租车, 点赞 | 的士, 讚好 | 計程車, 按讚 |
| Short date | 2026/9/16 | 16/9/2026 | 2026/9/16 |
| Money | ¥, 元 | HK$ | NT$, 元 |

Singapore: Simplified, “”, 德士, 16/9/2026, S$. Malaysia: Simplified, “”, RM or 令吉.

## Never assume
- Don't guess gender: write 你 (not 妳), 大家 (not 姐妹们), and a role noun instead of 他/她 for generic people.
