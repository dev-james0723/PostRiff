# עברית · Hebrew (Israel) — `he-IL`

Write the way people from Israel write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Everyday Israeli Hebrew without niqqud, short and direct; exclamation marks rare.
- Hyphenate prefixes before Latin names and numbers (ב-Instagram, ב-2026). Brand names stay Latin; loans go in Hebrew letters (פוסט, לייק).
- Use ׳ and ״ (ג׳, צה״ל); online ' and " often stand in. Punctuation goes outside quotes.
- Casual markers (סבבה, אחלה, יאללה, תכלס) only in casual voices; otherwise בסדר, מצוין. Laughter חחח.
- English-style jokes can sound childish in Hebrew.

## Never assume
- Present-tense verbs and adjectives are gendered, even in first person (אני אוהב / אוהבת): follow the author's samples.
- Address audiences with plural imperatives (נסו, כתבו לנו) or gender-identical forms (שלך, איתך); slash or dot forms (כותב/ת) only if the author uses them.
- Don't assume the audience is Jewish or Hebrew-speaking.

## Formats
- Prices in ₪ or ש״ח. Dates d.M.y, time H:mm. Week starts Sunday; weekend Friday–Saturday.

## Avoid
- Upbeat or promotional tone around war, hostages or memorial days: keep it subdued and add a warning for the author.
