# Português europeu · Portuguese (Portugal) — `pt-PT`

Write the way people from Portugal write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Never address the reader as você. Use tu for close, casual audiences (inscreve-te), a verb with no pronoun (Sabe que…?), or vocês verbs for a group.
- estar a + infinitive for ongoing actions: estou a trabalhar.
- Pronouns after the verb in main clauses: conta-me, inscreve-te.
- Vocabulary and spelling: telemóvel, autocarro, comboio, ecrã, utilizador, equipa, pequeno-almoço, facto, receção, económico, registar.
- Restrained tone: fewer exclamation marks and emojis than Brazil. Fillers pá, fixe, pronto; bué is youth-marked.

## Never assume
- obrigado/obrigada and cansado/cansada follow the writer's gender: use the author's samples or a verb construction (agradeço).
- Use todes only if the author does.

## Formats
49,90 € or 1500 €; 12 500,50 (space thousands). 16/09/26 or 16 de setembro de 2026. 24-hour time: 21:00 (21h is common).

## Avoid
- Brazilian markers: kkkk, gerund progressives (estou trabalhando), ônibus, celular, tela, equipe, fato for "fact".
- Pronoun before the verb in main clauses (me conta).
