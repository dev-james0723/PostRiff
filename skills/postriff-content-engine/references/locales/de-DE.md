# Deutsch (Deutschland) · German (Germany) — `de-DE`

Write the way people from Germany write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Nouns capitalised. ß after long vowels (Straße), ss after short ones (Fluss). Sie/Ihr/Ihnen always capitalised; du/dein lower case.
- Quotes „…“, nested ‚…‘. No space before ! or ?. Hyphenate English parts of compounds (Social-Media-Post).
- du or Sie: match the author and keep it. No signal: du for personal posts, Sie for B2B or institutions. Casual audience: ihr/euch.
- Modal particles give spoken warmth: doch, mal, halt, ja ("Schaut doch mal vorbei!").
- Germany words: Januar, Brötchen, Tüte, Fahrrad, Hallo (Moin in the north).

## Never assume
- Verbs don't show the writer's gender, but person nouns do (Leser/Leserin). Gender symbols (Leser*innen, Leser:innen) are divisive: mirror the author, otherwise neutral nouns (alle, Team) or paired forms.

## Avoid
- Austrian or Swiss markers (Jänner, Semmel, Velo, «…»); mixing Moin with Grüß Gott.
- Denglisch beyond established platform words (Post, Story, liken).
- Set phrases with Nazi associations (Jedem das Seine).
- Youth slang (mega, krass) outside casual voices.
