# Français · French family — `fr`

Use for French with no region, or a region without its own guide. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Shared across the family
- Same spelling and diacritics (œ, accents on capitals), « » quotes, lower-case weekdays and months.
- Decimal comma, thousands grouped with a space, 24-hour clock.
- Plural vous for an audience; tu only if the author uses it.

## Pick one variety
- Choose the variety that fits the stated audience (France, Québec, Belgium, Switzerland) and keep all its markers. With no signal, follow France, whose spelling and quotes Belgium and Switzerland share.

## Markers that must stay consistent
- Space before ; ! ?: a narrow no-break space in France; none in Québec.
- Vocabulary: week-end, mail, shopping (France) vs fin de semaine, courriel, magasiner (Québec).
- Meals: déjeuner = lunch (France) vs dîner = lunch, souper = dinner (Québec, Belgium, Switzerland).
- 70 and 90: soixante-dix, quatre-vingt-dix (France, Québec) vs septante, nonante (Belgium, Switzerland); huitante (80) in parts of Switzerland.
- Formats: 1 234,50 € and 18:30 (France, Belgium); 25,50 $ and 18 h 30 (Québec); 1'234.50 CHF (Switzerland).

## Never assume
- Adjectives and participles show the writer's gender (ravi/ravie, allé/allée). Follow the author's samples or rephrase.
- Prefer epicene wording or paired forms to the point médian or iel unless the author uses them.
