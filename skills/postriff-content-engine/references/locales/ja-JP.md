# 日本語 · Japanese — `ja-JP`

Write the way people from Japan write their own social posts in this variety. These are general conventions for the variety, not facts about the author: the author's VOICE.md decides tone and habits, and the approved facts decide content.

## Decisive markers
- No spaces between words; loanwords in katakana, keeping the long-vowel mark (コンピューター).
- Full-width 、。！？. 「」 for quotations and highlighted words, 『』 for titles of works.
- One style per post: です/ます by default, plain だ/である only when the author's samples use it.
- Endings carry the voice: 〜ね shares a feeling, 〜よ tells, 〜かな muses, 〜かも hedges, 〜です！ sounds bright.
- Drop the subject as Japanese naturally does. Line breaks set the pace on Instagram and LINE.
- Foreign names in established katakana, joined with ・.

## Words that give the region away
| Write | Not |
|---|---|
| 、。！？ | , . ! ? (half-width) |
| コンピューター | コンピュータ (drops the long vowel) |
| 私, or no subject | 僕 / 俺 / うち (assigns gender and persona) |
| 笑 (casual voice) | （笑） (dated); 草 / w in a polite voice |
| ご覧ください / ありがとうございます | 見てね / ありがとう〜 in the same post (mixed styles) |
| だよね | ダヨネ (katakana endings, mocked as おじさん構文) |
| 3,000円 or ¥3,000 | ¥3,000円 (both marks) |

## Tone, particles and address
- Understatement works; a humble-brag (自虐風自慢) is disliked. Frame promotion as gratitude and invitation: ご報告です。…本当にありがとうございます。
- Soften with ね and よ. At most about three emoji; heavy emoji, kaomoji and stickers top the list of mocked おじさん構文 traits.
- Address the audience as 皆さん or 皆さま, customers as お客様.
- LINE official accounts: short, soft (not stiff), image-led. Instagram: hashtags at the end. X: short, few hashtags. note: a titled essay.

## Language mixing
- Katakana loanwords are normal; overusing them reads as おじさん構文.
- Spacing around Latin text varies; follow the samples.

## Never assume
- First-person pronouns signal gender, age and persona: 私 (neutral, polite), 僕 (male-coded, soft), 俺 (male, casual), うち (casual, young). Never choose 僕, 俺 or うち for the author; drop the subject or use 私 unless the samples show otherwise.
- Take plain vs polite style and sentence endings from the author's samples.
- Don't assume the author's age, relationship or location.

## Formats
- Dates: 9月16日(水) or 2026年9月16日; era years (令和8年) mostly in official documents. Times: 19:00.
- Prices: 3,000円 or ¥3,000; consumer prices as tax-inclusive totals, e.g. 3,300円（税込）.

## Local rules to flag, not to enforce
- Paid promotion must be recognisable as an ad (stealth-marketing rules, since October 2023): if a post looks sponsored with no clear ad label, add a warning for the author.
- Businesses must show consumers tax-inclusive totals: if a price looks pre-tax, add a warning for the author.

## Avoid
- Mixing です/ます and plain だ in one post.
- （笑）, katakana-heavy endings, stacks of emoji or kaomoji.
- 私は in every sentence (reads translated).
- Favourable comparison with named peers.
