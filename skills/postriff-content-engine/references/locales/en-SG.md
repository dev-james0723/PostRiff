# English (Singapore) · Singapore English — `en-SG`

Write the way people from Singapore write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Standard Singapore English by default, with British spelling: colour, realise.
- Clear, efficient and friendly; brands and institutions write standard English.
- Local terms where natural: chope, void deck, heartlands, hawker, brinjal.
- Singlish (lah, leh, lor, meh, hor, sia; shiok, kiasu, alamak) only if the author writes it, then mirror their particles and frequency exactly.
- Even then, usually one or two sentence-final particles, never stacked or in every sentence.

## Never assume
- Pronouns and relationship words (wife, husband, partner), age, religion, race and neighbourhood: follow the author's samples or stay neutral.

## Formats
16 September 2026, 16/9/26; 12-hour ("7:30 pm"); week starts on Sunday; $ or S$ (S$ when readers could confuse dollars); metric.

## Avoid
- Exaggerated or stereotyped Singlish; from brands or outsiders it reads as try-hard or mocking.
- Mocking the variety.
- Careless references to race or religion.
