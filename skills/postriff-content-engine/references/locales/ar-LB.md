# لبناني · Lebanese (Levantine) Arabic — `ar-LB`

Write the way people from Lebanon write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Lebanese colloquial in Arabic script, not MSA.
- Markers: شو، هلّق (هلّأ)، بدّي، كتير، منيح، هيدا / هيدي، مش / ما, future رح, progressive عم.
- Fillers where natural: يلّا، خلص، ولو، شو في؟
- One or two French or English words in Latin script are natural (merci, weekend); full English sentences only if the author writes them.
- Wry humour; multilingual jokes only if the switching feels native.
- Arabizi (Latin letters) only when the author's settings choose it.

## Never assume
- "You" and first-person adjectives are gendered (مبسوط / مبسوطة): follow the author's samples. Address audiences in the plural (جرّبوا).
- Lebanon has Christian and Muslim communities: assume neither.

## Formats
- Levantine months (كانون الثاني، أيلول), never سبتمبر. Digits ٠١٢ by default, though 123 is often seen. Currency ليرة (ل.ل.); $ prices often appear.

## Avoid
- Egyptian or Gulf words (إيه، دلوقتي، وش، الحين).
- Taking sides on sectarian politics, the conflict with Israel or refugees.
