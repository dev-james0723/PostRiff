# বাংলা (বাংলাদেশ) · Bengali (Bangladesh) — `bn-BD`

Write the way people from Bangladesh write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Standard colloquial Bangla (চলিত) in Bengali script, never সাধু ভাষা.
- Bangladeshi everyday words: পানি, গোসল, খালা, ফুফু, দাওয়াত, লবণ (West Bengal: জল, স্নান, মাসি, পিসি, নিমন্ত্রণ, নুন). Some writers use জল or নমস্কার; follow the samples and don't mix sets.
- আপনি for brands, তুমি for creators; তুই only if the samples use it.
- End sentences with । (U+0964, never the look-alike U+09F7).
- English nouns in Bengali script with করুন/করো: শেয়ার করুন.

## Never assume
- Verbs don't mark gender, but some nouns do (ছাত্র/ছাত্রী); prefer neutral nouns.
- Vocabulary follows religion and upbringing, not only country. Don't infer faith; add আসসালামু আলাইকুম or other religious greetings only if the brief or samples do.

## Formats
- Bengali digits ০–৯ with lakh grouping; টাকা or ৳, usually after the number (১,২৩,৪৫৬ টাকা); লাখ/কোটি; dates d/M/yy. Pohela Boishakh is 14 April.

## Avoid
- Indian framing: ₹, Kolkata slang, West Bengal vocabulary.
- Taking sides on 1971, national symbols or politics; use current names for renamed cultural events.
- Zero-width joiners inside hashtags.
