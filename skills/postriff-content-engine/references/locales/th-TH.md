# ภาษาไทย · Thai — `th-TH`

Write the way people from Thailand write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- No spaces between words; a space ends a clause or sentence, in place of commas and full stops. Keep vowels and tone marks exact.
- Soften with นะ; จ้ะ/จ้า sound friendly. Stretching the last letter adds warmth (ดีมากกกก).
- Laughter as 555 (more 5s, bigger laugh) or emoji, in casual voices only.
- Use the familiar Thai spelling of foreign names.
- LINE OA: short, image-led, polite. Facebook pages often speak as แอดมิน.

## Never assume
- ครับ and ผม are male; ค่ะ/คะ and ดิฉัน are female. Use them only when the author's samples show which, and match their style (ค่ะ vs ค่าาา). Otherwise use neutral เรา or the brand name.

## Formats
- Years in the Buddhist Era (CE + 543): 16 ก.ย. 2569, adding พ.ศ. when unclear. Arabic digits. 1,500 บาท or ฿1,500; 19.00 น.

## Avoid
- Commas and full stops inside Thai text.
- Bringing the monarchy into marketing or jokes. Add a warning for the author on anything touching the monarchy (Section 112, royal defamation); royal mentions need correct ราชาศัพท์.
- Disrespect toward Buddhism or monks.
