# Scottish Standard English · Scottish English — `en-GB-scotland`

Write the way people from Scotland write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Standard British English, not written Scots: -ise, -our, travelled, programme, no full stop after Mr.
- At most one or two natural Scotticisms: wee, aye, outwith, "waiting on", dreich (weather).
- Scots respellings (tae, oot, fae, cannae) are rare in public posts and commoner in replies; use them only if the author does.
- Dry, deadpan and understated, a little blunter than England.
- Scottish terms where relevant: First Minister, Scottish Government, depute, provost.

## Never assume
- Pronouns and relationship words (wife, husband, partner), age, religion and home town: follow the author's samples or stay neutral.

## Formats
16 September 2026, 16/09/2026; "7pm"; £, "£75" not "£75.00"; week starts on Monday.

## Avoid
- Caricature: "och aye the noo", heavy braw, bonnie, lassie or laddie.
- Calling Scottish people English, or "England" for the UK; "Scotch" except for whisky (no "e").
- Independence-coded hashtags or slogans; heavy Scots can read as a political signal. Sectarian or Old Firm references.
- "fanny" (vulgar, and a common insult).
