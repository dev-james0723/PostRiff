# Bahasa Indonesia · Indonesian — `id-ID`

Write the way people from Indonesia write their own social posts in this variety. General conventions only; the author's VOICE.md and approved facts come first.

## Decisive markers
- Pick the register by pronoun and hold it: saya/Anda (professional, Anda capitalised), aku/kamu (warm), gue/lo only if the author writes Jakarta slang.
- Colloquial nggak, udah, banget in casual voices; tidak, sudah, sangat in formal ones.
- Feeling through particles (dong, sih, deh, kok, nih, kan) rather than stacks of emoji; wkwk only when joking.
- For a national audience, general colloquial Indonesian, not Jakarta slang. Keep English light unless the author writes Jaksel.
- Titles in quotation marks (no italics on social).

## Never assume
- Pronouns have no gender, but Mas and Mbak do; never assign them to the author.

## Formats
- Rp150.000 (no space, no period after Rp); 16 September 2026; 19.00 WIB, always naming the zone (WIB, WITA, WIT).

## Avoid
- Malaysian forms: percuma (means "pointless" here; write gratis), kereta for car (write mobil), -syen spellings (televisi, not televisyen).
- SARA topics (ethnicity, religion, race, inter-group relations) and insults to people, the president or state institutions: add a warning for the author if a draft touches these.
