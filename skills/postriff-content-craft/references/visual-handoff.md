# Visual and spoken handoff

Use the selected brand, exact native format and current social-graphics contract.
This reference supplies editorial direction; it does not generate or approve media.

For a carousel, draft the full sequence before producing individual cards. Assign
each card one job: establish the question, add context, explain/show evidence,
express the creator's supplied observation or land the argument. Put a useful point early.
Remove any card whose deletion loses nothing. Keep sources and qualifications
attached to claims. Cover and caption share one promise without duplicating all
the text. The story chooses its slide count. Draft ordered slide text in notes
when PostRiff's selected format needs it; the caption stays audience-facing copy.

For a YouTube title/thumbnail pair, state what each contributes. Pick a single
focal subject and an emotionally truthful treatment. Consider up to three concepts
only when the choice matters. Judge at a small phone/feed preview; exact dimensions
come from the target's current spec. No universal four-word limit, shocked face,
oversized metric or blanket ban on shared title words. A calm piece of music can
have a calm image. Use genuine performance frames and real UI when documenting
the creator's work. A conceptual illustration must be recognisable as conceptual.

For TikTok/Reels/Shorts, write separate fields for speech, editorial on-screen
headline, accessibility captions and the real/proposed opening visual. Spoken and
headline text may differ to divide their jobs; verbatim accessibility captions
remain valid. Name asset gaps and approximate performance timing honestly. Preserve
musical pauses and coherent thought; no compulsory fast cuts or looping gimmick.

Pass the selected target/language, visual thesis, ordered text, media source,
rights notes, evidence/caveats, alt-text needs and title/cover relationship to
`postriff-social-graphics`. That skill owns static assets, brand selection, Guizang
eligibility and separate mechanical/visual/factual/localization/accessibility/rights
validation. Route finished motion through the existing video workflow. If PostRiff
cannot see supplied image bytes, it must not claim to have inspected their contents.

Text-only is a valid choice. No decorative quote card is necessary to complete a
post. Recompose separate languages and aspect ratios, keeping their claim identical
in strength. A draft visual brief is never a produced asset or post approval.
