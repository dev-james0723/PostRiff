# Editorial workflow

These are craft choices, not algorithm facts. Load the existing Content Engine
first; this document does not create a second persona or personal story bank.

## Source before structure

Use supplied source notes, approved examples and James's already supplied angle.
Record the one thesis, intended reader, desired takeaway and evidence supporting
it. Separate sourced facts from James's observation and from another speaker's
opinion. For a neutral summary, retain attribution without adding first-person
beliefs. Reuse known answers; do not mine unrelated files or memories for stories.

A concrete detail can be a musical phrase, a design decision, a real object or a
specific question. It does not need a number or uncomfortable personal disclosure.
If the only material is a generic topic and a personal story is necessary, ask
one question about an actual moment. Ask a clarifying follow-up only if useful,
accept the answer, and preserve limits on naming people. No new persistent story
bank is created; use the existing campaign notes and conversation state. Cross-
campaign reuse of private details needs an explicitly designated source.

## Choose an opening that the material earns

Consider a concrete observation, a practical problem, a real choice/trade-off,
an honest question, or a verified result. Select the best fit without forcing the
user through a hook menu. Offer at most three materially different options when
requested or when the choice changes the angle. Question-led openings are allowed.
Do not turn a cautious idea into certainty, a calm story into conflict, or a
source's result into James's achievement. Do not hide an essential caveat behind
a curiosity gap. The opening's promise must be fulfilled by the body or actual video.

## Rebuild the delivery from the meaning

Write the core thought in James's voice, then adapt independently by target and
language. Keep the central claim, evidence, qualifications and attribution stable.
Change context, pacing, length, examples and media roles as needed. Remove obsolete
platform references, wrong handles and irrelevant CTAs; retain useful citations,
disclosures and source credit. Do not copy a master caption and trim it seven ways.
An English LinkedIn paragraph and a Cantonese-facing Threads observation should
feel native to their audiences without claiming different facts.

Length follows substance and current adapter constraints. A concise reflection
can remain short; a thoughtful argument may need paragraphs. No fixed daily quota,
post length, sentence pattern, slide count, hashtag count or posting window applies.
Choose one next action only when it helps the reader; a complete observation may
end with no CTA. Keep needed source/product links visible and correctly labelled.
Never create a first-comment or reply job simply to relocate a link for reach.

## Proportionate review

Read as James talking to one thoughtful person. Replace vague praise with an
existing detail or a more precise statement; do not add facts to make text vivid.
Remove corporate boilerplate, repeated dramatic fragments, mechanical triads,
engagement bait and generic inspiration when they distract. Preserve intentional
punctuation, natural Cantonese, literary rhythm, humour and honest uncertainty.
Do not insert typos, hedges, sensory detail, confession or abrupt fragments to
simulate humanity. If the user requests verbatim copy, retain it and report
material issues separately. No detector scoring or claims of detector evasion.

Check the opening against the payoff; check facts, names, dates and links against
sources; check language and selected native format; check voice, privacy and media
needs. Missing facts remain omitted or explicitly unresolved. A voice edit cannot
be reported as a factual check. A known issue must be visible in warnings.

In Studio, `copy` contains audience-facing text. `notes` briefly explains the
specific adaptation and missing assets. Keep internal skill IDs, policy text and
source file paths out of the public caption. Existing instruction hashes make
the generation review traceable. Learning from later performance requires real
comparable observations; a single success does not validate an algorithm rule.
