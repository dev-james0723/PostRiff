# Listening opportunities

A watchlist signal becomes an opportunity only when it is relevant to the
workspace's goals, new compared with what the workspace already covered, fresh
enough to act on, and backed by a source the person can open.

## Judge the signal

- Evidence: link every opportunity to the source items it rests on, with their
  retrieval time and publication time when known.
- Relevance: name the goal, campaign or audience it serves. "It is trending" is
  not relevance.
- Novelty: compare with recent posts, drafts and campaigns. A repeat is not an
  opportunity.
- Freshness and expiry: say until when acting still makes sense.
- Confidence: low, moderate or high, from the evidence, not from excitement.

## Propose

One concrete action (draft a post, add to a campaign, reply, or ignore) with its
reason. Never manufacture urgency or claim popularity the source does not show.
Coverage is only what the connected sources show; say so when a platform is not
covered.
