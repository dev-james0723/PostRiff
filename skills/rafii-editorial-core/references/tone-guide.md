# Tone guide

A tone changes how one message feels to its reader. It changes no fact, no
claim and no language. Each entry gives the rule, the moves that usually carry
the tone, and what to leave alone. The examples are invented.

## Warm

Sound kind and close to the reader. Use plain, considerate wording and speak to the reader directly where the text already does. Do not add personal stories, feelings, praise or thanks that the text does not contain.

- Moves: plainer words for stiff ones, "you" and "we" where the text already
  addresses someone, a softer request ("please bring" for "attendees must
  bring"), shorter sentences where a long one sounds cold.
- Leave alone: every condition and deadline, however firm. Warm is not vague.
- Not warm, just added: "We are so excited", "Thank you so much for your
  support", "It means the world to us", when the text said none of it.
- Chinese: 改用較親切的字眼和語氣，例如「請」、「歡迎」，但不要加入原文沒有的感受
  （「好開心」、「非常感謝」）。稱呼沿用原文的「你」或「您」。

## Professional

Sound composed and exact. Prefer complete sentences and precise wording. Do not make the text stiff, longer or more formal than its platform needs, and keep a first-person voice that is already there.

- Moves: complete sentences for fragments, the exact word for the loose one,
  consistent terms, fewer exclamation marks and intensifiers.
- Leave alone: "I" and "we". Professional is not impersonal, and it is not the
  passive voice.
- Not professional, just heavier: "utilise", "in order to", "we are pleased to
  announce that", long nominal chains, a summary sentence added at the end.
- Chinese: 用語準確、句子完整即可。不必把口語全部改成公文語氣；
  粵語書寫的帖文仍然用粵語，只收斂過於隨意的語氣詞。

## Direct

Say the main point early and plainly. Cut preamble and qualifiers that carry no information. Keep every hedge, condition and limit that changes what is claimed.

- Moves: lead with the point, drop throat-clearing ("I just wanted to", "It is
  worth noting that"), one verb for a phrase, active voice where the actor is
  known.
- Leave alone: "may", "about", "so far", "if", "unless", "only". These carry
  meaning. "Probably on Friday" does not become "on Friday".
- Not direct, just blunt: removing "please", turning a request into an order,
  dropping the reason the text gave.
- Chinese: 先講重點，刪去鋪墊。保留「可能」、「大約」、「如果」、「暫定」這些會改變
  意思的字眼。

## Playful

Sound light and good-humoured in a way that fits the subject and the reader. Do not joke about facts, do not mock anyone, and do not add emoji, slang or exclamation marks the author does not already use.

- Moves: a lighter word, a livelier verb, a shorter rhythm, a small turn of
  phrase built from what the text already says.
- Leave alone: serious subjects. If the text is about a loss, a complaint, a
  safety matter or money owed, a playful tone does not fit; return the text
  unchanged and say why.
- Not playful, just risky: a joke that changes a number or a date, a pun on a
  person's or product's name, irony a reader could take literally.
- Chinese: 語氣可以輕鬆些，但不要加入作者本來不用的表情符號、潮語或語氣詞。

## Custom

Follow the person's own tone instruction. It can change attitude only; the rules about what must stay still apply.

- Read the instruction as a description of attitude ("calmer", "less salesy",
  "like a note to a colleague").
- If it asks for something a tone cannot be (a shorter text, another language,
  a new claim, a different audience's facts), apply the attitude part and say
  in the warning or note field what was not done.
- An instruction that cannot be read as a tone at all leaves the text
  unchanged.

## Depth and tone together

- **Light:** Fix concrete friction only and leave the rest as written. Returning the text unchanged is a valid result.
- **Standard:** Recast sentences that read awkwardly or generically. Keep every substantive detail, the order of ideas and the emphasis.
- **Deep:** Only when the person asks for a strong rewrite. Rebuild the prose from its meaning. Keep every distinct claim and its logical dependencies, and keep the order unless a change was requested.

At Light, a tone change touches the few words that set the attitude and
nothing else. At Standard, whole sentences may be recast. Deep is for a text
whose tone is far from the one asked for.

## Mixed-language text

Some authors mix languages on purpose. Keep each language where it is: an
English term inside a Chinese sentence stays English, and a Chinese phrase
inside an English post stays Chinese. Apply the tone inside each language with
that language's own means. Do not make the text uniform.
