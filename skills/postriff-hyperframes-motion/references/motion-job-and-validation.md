# Motion job and validation contract

## Workflow routing

`faceless-explainer` is the default for approved articles, transcript-derived briefs, topics, and teaching/argument content between 30 and 180 seconds. `motion-graphics` is reserved for an unnarrated, motion-first unit shorter than ten seconds. Other durations or route combinations remain blocked until the design policy changes.

## Native variants

| Target family | Aspect ratio | Planning dimensions |
|---|---|---|
| `youtube_or_embed` | 16:9 | 1920x1080 |
| `linkedin_x_instagram_feed` | 1:1 | 1080x1080 |
| `shorts_reels_tiktok` | 9:16 | 1080x1920 |

Each selected family receives its own composition, safe-area, text-density, caption, cover, accessibility, visual, audio, and rights review. Shared meaning does not permit mechanical cropping.

## Provenance

Pin the HyperFrames version and retain source artifact IDs/hashes, brief hash, storyboard hash, composition hash, selected template, caption/translation refs, media-rights refs, and output asset IDs/hashes when rendering is separately authorized.

## State separation

`planned`, `brief_ready`, `storyboard_review`, `composition_ready`, `validating`, `rendered`, `uploaded`, `scheduled`, `published`, and `verified` are independent milestones. A structural lint/check/inspect pass is not visual or audio approval. A local MP4 is not remote media and has no upload, scheduling, publishing, or verification authority.
