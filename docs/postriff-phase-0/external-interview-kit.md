# External interview kit — ready to use, not sent

Date: 2026-09-14. Execution state: local preparation. No participants selected, contacted, consented, booked or interviewed by this task. No recruitment fees or incentives authorized. This kit reuses the existing [interview guide](interview-guide.md) and [participant roster](participants.md).

## Four recruitment slots

| Slot | Useful perspective | Eligibility to establish, not assume |
|---|---|---|
| P02 | Independent creator or educator; preferably non-technical | Personally creates or seriously attempts social posts; can discuss recent work. |
| P03 | Solo subject expert; preferably an existing agent/CLI user | Can describe actual tools and setup, including failures; installation alone is not agent use. |
| P04 | Founder or operator of a small business | Personally makes content or owns the actual source-to-post workflow. |
| P05 | Multilingual creator, or a person filling a missing perspective | Can explain real audience/language adaptation, or another underrepresented target situation. |

Seek four distinct external people covering at least three situations. Do not screen for liking PostRiff or already having the hypothesized problem. A smooth workflow, rare need or rejection is valuable evidence. Prefer participants beyond close founder collaborators where practical. Record any relationship/bias without personal identifiers.

## Invitation candidates

Use one language appropriate to the recipient. These messages are unsent. James can send one himself. Assistant sending needs an identified recipient/channel and explicit approval of the exact message; no approval is requested until a target is available.

**English**

Hi! I’m researching how independent creators, educators and small-business owners turn ideas or source material into social posts. Would you be open to a 35–50 minute conversation about a recent post you made or tried to make? I’d like to hear what you actually did, what worked and what didn’t, then get your reaction to a possible tool. It’s research, not a sales call, and you don’t need to like the idea. You can skip any question and keep private material to yourself. I’d ask permission for written notes; there’s no recording. An asynchronous text conversation also works if that’s easier.

**繁體中文**

你好！我正在了解獨立創作者、教育工作者和小型企業經營者，平日怎樣把想法或資料變成社交平台內容。想邀請你用大約 35–50 分鐘，聊聊最近一次做過、或認真嘗試做的貼文：實際做了甚麼、哪些地方順利、哪些地方有困難，之後再聽聽你對一個工具構想的看法。這是研究，並非推銷，你不需要認同這個構想。任何問題都可以跳過，亦不用提供私人資料。我會先徵求你同意才做文字筆記，不會錄音。如果文字對答比較方便，也可以分次進行。

## Intake before substantive questions

Ask briefly and sequentially; use an anonymous ID in research files.

1. What kind of content do you personally make, and for whom?
2. Approximately when did you last create or seriously attempt a social post?
3. Which platforms/languages do you use, and which AI tools, if any, do you actually use?
4. Which language and conversation format would be comfortable for you?

Someone without a recent example can still offer feedback, but that alone cannot satisfy the recent-work/recurrence gates. Mark incomplete coverage rather than excluding contrary evidence.

## Consent opening

Before notes: “Is it okay if I keep written notes under a participant code for this product research? You can skip questions or ask me not to retain a detail. I won’t record this session or publish identifiable quotes without separate permission. Please leave out credentials, private client details and other sensitive material.”

If AI analysis will be used, additionally explain and obtain agreement: “I would also like to use an AI assistant to help organize the anonymized notes. That may send the selected notes to its model provider. Are you comfortable with that?”

Record local-note permission and AI-processing permission separately. If AI processing is declined or unknown, keep the notes out of Codex/provider prompts and use a human-only interview workflow until a separately permitted summary is available. Do not imply that a local file means all subsequent AI processing stays on the laptop. No recording, private upload or public attribution is included in either consent question.

## Interview execution

Use the [existing guide](interview-guide.md) one question at a time. Let the participant describe actual work before showing the concept or pricing. Do not send them the founder's answers as examples. Do not show the local alpha before the initial story unless explicitly conducting a separate usability study.

Capture the real task, approximate date, trigger, actual sequence, tools, final outcome and an earlier comparable task. Probe one unclear step at a time. Record timing as measured, estimated, unknown or skipped; separate active and elapsed time and avoid double counting edits. Ask for optional before/after wording without demanding private files.

After the story, show the concept steps in section B of the guide sequentially, then memory/privacy questions, then proposed pricing. Explain that the local prototype does not establish real agent execution, hosted identity, automated publication or saved time. P02/P04 hear Assist first; P03/P05 hear Studio first. Both get equal detail. Plans and trials are research hypotheses; no checkout or signup is offered.

At the end, read back the factual summary and ask for corrections. An assistant-authored summary is not a direct quote. Preserve any later corrections and the participant's actual uncertainty.

## Existing-note intake

If interviews already happened, first establish participant uniqueness, target situation, session date/window, interviewer and permission for local notes/AI analysis. Keep only permitted, relevant, anonymized material. Do not ask for an entire private chat archive. Link each observation to its source and distinguish exact quotations, paraphrases and interpretations. Missing concept, timing or recurrence answers require follow-up; do not backfill them from founder opinions.

## Per-session evidence checklist

- Participant ID, distinct-person confirmation, role/situation, relationship/bias, language and session date/window.
- Local notes and AI-processing consent: granted / declined / unknown, with wording/date.
- RQ01–RQ12 coverage with evidence IDs; unknowns and contradictions visible.
- A real recent task plus recurrence; baseline and method, or explicit missing/skipped fields.
- Actual source use, voice edits, decisions and privacy expectations; optional artifacts only within permission.
- Concept reactions, remembered-preference teach-back, expected return occasion and concrete pricing/trial reactions. Desired behavior is separate from observed behavior.
- Participant factual corrections, remaining follow-ups and completion assessment.

Update the existing P02–P05 records only from actual responses, then update the matrix and roster. Keep the matrix at 12 questions × 5 participants; multiple observations never create extra participants. Do not mark a complete interview solely because every heading has text.

## Resuming Phase 2

After the missing founder task evidence and four external interviews are collected, synthesize the actual findings and apply every [Phase 0 exit gate](phase-0-plan.md#exit-gates). A final proceed or approved-revise decision must define scope and measurable acceptance; a revise decision may require a Phase 1 delta. Then run the Phase 1 engineering baseline required by the Phase 2 prompt. The user's instruction to start Phase 2 afterward remains in force if these conditions pass; no repeat approval for that same local scope is needed. External deployment, account changes, paid requests and publishing still require their exact action approvals.
