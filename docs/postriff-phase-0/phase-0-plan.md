## Hypotheses and disconfirming evidence

| ID | Hypothesis to investigate | Evidence that would weaken it |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Creators repeatedly struggle to turn existing knowledge or sources into usable posts | Task is rare, easily completed, or content creation is not a priority |
| H2 | Adapting to multiple channels, languages or audiences adds meaningful work | Participants use only one channel or identical copy is sufficient |
| H3 | Approved examples and corrections improve recognizable voice and reduce editing | Most edits concern missing facts or strategy; remembering preferences adds little value |
| H4 | Guided setup with optional modes fits the way people describe their work | Mode labels confuse, force artificial categories, or delay value |
| H5 | People can specify useful durable preferences and understand privacy boundaries | They cannot predict what is remembered, who sees it, or how to undo it |
| H6 | Existing-agent and managed-AI choices serve distinct setup preferences | CLI setup costs dominate; agent access alone is available elsewhere and sufficient |
| H7 | A valuable recurring outcome justifies testing $19 Studio / $39 Studio Assist and trials | They would use only free export/scheduling, reject limits, or cannot identify a return occasion |

## Evidence protocol

Use one real recent task first; establish recurrence with another occurrence or a stated cadence and its time window. Hear the story before presenting PostRiff. Ask one question at a time. Separate direct quote (exact words), participant report, observed artifact, interpretation, and recommendation. Label translated quotes and retain the original. Record contradictions rather than resolving them by assumption. Founder history is supporting context only and is not prefilled as an interview answer.

Give observations stable IDs such as P01-E01. Store a question ID, note anchor, task/date or date uncertainty, evidence type, participant statement or observation, source reference, and correction status. Research-question rows can share an observation ID but never inflate participant counts. Missing evidence is unknown, not a negative answer. Confidence is none / low / medium / high with a reason; it describes evidence for that row, not market certainty.

Record active time separately from elapsed time. Preserve reported ranges and approximations. Label measured, participant estimate, or unknown. Parallel work cannot be added twice. Record source checking, drafting, adaptation and AI correction without double counting corrections already included in drafting. Tool spend is optional. Prices and trial choices are stated preferences until a real later action occurs; a free-trial choice is neither signup nor purchase.

Keep interview notes local, anonymous except the explicitly named founder, and limited to relevant content. No credentials, private account identifiers, contact details, unnecessary sensitive facts, original skill text, or confidential documents in the package. Agree separately on any external sharing or retention terms before recruitment expands. No participant-specific input goes into a generalized skill template.

## Exit gates

Recommend proceed only after all five real interviews and all the following:

1. At least 3/5 demonstrate the repeated source-to-post problem using recent work. Count only sessions with concrete workflow evidence and recurrence; track missing recurrence separately.
2. Evidence identifies a coherent first segment and workflow; do not average incompatible jobs together.
3. A baseline exists for the selected workflow to compare time or editing reduction. Preserve unknowns and collection method; no fabricated precision.
4. At least 3/5 can explain what PostRiff should remember about them.
5. No unresolved privacy flaw in the private-source / neutral-template / customer-instance model. Participant comprehension alone does not prove technical isolation; implementation must later validate it.
6. A testable reason to choose PostRiff beyond generic scheduling or agent access.
7. A clear first-return occasion.
8. Concrete objections to trial terms and prices, including rejection reasons; hypothetical interest remains hypothetical.

If gates fail after evidence is collected, choose revise or stop with specific supporting observations. Missing interviews mean decision pending, not failed demand. A positive decision authorizes no Phase 1 code by itself.

## Risks and bounded responses

Founder bias: assess P02–P05 separately. Selection bias: recruit across situations and include people dissatisfied with the concept. Leading/price anchoring: hear the current workflow before showing the concept; present both offers neutrally and alternate their order across interviews. Recall bias: keep ranges, seek optional artifacts, and do not translate confidence into precise time. Scope drift: no source edits, account setup, model runs, deployment, billing or publication in Phase 0. Small sample: identify a testable segment; do not claim representative market demand.
