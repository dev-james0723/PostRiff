**Why S1 misses, from the mock's request log (the capacity agent verifies this):**

- [Certain] The lane works about 46 of every 60 seconds. Requests by second of the minute are zero for seconds 0 to 5 and fade out from second 51 to 55: duty cycle 77%. The lane budget is 50 s (`DEFAULT_SECONDS`), the runtime is built inside the request, and the next cron minute starts a new loop.
- [Certain] In the working seconds the admitted rate averages 21.3 requests a second, 85% of the ceiling of 25; the plateau average is 16.3.
- [Certain] The plateau (minutes 0 to 23) cost 6.73 requests per created post (23,568 requests for 3,502 creations); read-backs are served late, which is why create to verified is 18 minutes at median and a customer would see the post in Discord long before Rafii shows it as confirmed.
- [Likely] Median time is about 2,500 x 6.73 / (rate x 60) minutes: 11.2 at a perfect 25 requests a second, 17.2 at the observed 16.3 (observed 17.19).
- [Likely, a projection] Applying the levers in order: duty cycle 77% to 97% gives 13.6 min; packing 85% to 94% of the ceiling gives 12.3; one request less per post (5.73) gives 10.5; two less gives 8.7. The first two alone do not reliably meet the 12-minute median, so a request reduction, or a decision about what the 25 a second budget charges, is part of the answer.
- [Certain] After minute 33 only 44 to 67 posts a minute are created while about 14.7 requests a second still flow; this tail sets the 40.7-minute maximum (without it the plateau would have finished near minute 34). Cause under analysis.
