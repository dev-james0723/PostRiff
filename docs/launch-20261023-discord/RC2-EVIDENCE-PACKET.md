- [Certain] The lane works about 46 of every 60 seconds. Requests by second of the minute are zero for seconds 0 to 5 and fade out from second 51 to 55: duty cycle 77%. The lane budget is 50 s (`DEFAULT_SECONDS`), the runtime is built inside the request, and the next cron minute starts a new loop.
