Official push notifications are implemented as signed uploads/title/description hints, with explicit subscription review, encrypted verification/HMAC secrets and approved lease renewal. Each hint wakes official read-back and never proves publication.

Current quota defaults: dedicated videos.insert 100 calls/day at 1 unit each; dedicated search.list 100 calls/day; other Data API methods general 10000 units/day. Actual project allocation and remaining quota are unverified. Normative documentation/discovery override stale autogenerated page summaries.

Local validation is complete for the candidate: 220 focused Python tests; 19 creator PostgreSQL groups; 24 video PostgreSQL checks; campaign worker; typecheck; production build; lint with zero errors and three pre-existing warnings; desktop/mobile synthetic browser checks at 1440/390/320. **These are not real Google E2E.** See REAL-E2E-MATRIX.md and VALIDATION.json.

Rafii applies conservative 30-day evidence freshness, not a Google approval expiry. Unknown project evidence cannot be replaced with the historical REVIEWED boolean. Authorization maintenance refreshes the original channel/grant at most once per bounded tick, with a successful cache of at most 30 minutes or less near access-token expiry.

The initial matrix is historical evidence of Stage 1. Its initial podcast artwork limit is superseded by the current documented 50 MB API media contract above; Rafii image ingestion remains capped at 8 MB.
