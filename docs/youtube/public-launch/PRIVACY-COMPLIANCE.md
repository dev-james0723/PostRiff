## Required behavior and acceptance gaps

The official [YouTube Developer Policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies) distinguish:

- In-app revocation: remote token revocation immediately; associated Authorized Data removed as soon as possible and within seven calendar days.
- Revocation through Google permissions: periodically detect lost authorization and remove related API Data as soon as possible, within 30 days.
- User/account deletion requests: related stored data removed within seven days; original YouTube content is separate.
- Non-statistical metadata/comments: refresh or delete within 30 days. Authorized statistics can have different retention conditions, including recurring authorization/deleted-video checks. The current shorter 30-day cache policy is conservative, but disclosure alone does not prove cleanup.

**Concrete baseline gap:** `OAuthService._shared_elsewhere` at `a14c5b28` defers remote YouTube revoke whenever any other Google grant is active, including other workspaces; baseline legal pages truthfully disclose that deferral. The disclosure does not satisfy an actual consent-revocation control's immediate-revoke requirement. Preserve a clearly named local connection-removal control, provide an actual Google authorization-revocation path with impact disclosure and verified result, and avoid exposing unrelated tenants. A dedicated YouTube project isolates Gmail/Calendar coupling but does not alone prove independent grants per Brand channel. The engineering owner must resolve this behavior and demonstrate cleanup before asserting compliance. Attach the repair/ref and acceptance evidence here when completed; this baseline finding is not a claim that later fixes are absent.

Additional proof needed: pruning of YouTube material in operation receipts, audit JSON, caches, thumbnails/profile images, reporting imports, AI context/derived material and backups; purge even while creator flags are off; retry/failure handling; revoked-token detection; production maintenance cadence; deletion request and completion timestamps; preservation of content-free tombstones only. Do not erase operational uncertainty by promising a deadline the deployed system has not demonstrated.

## Derived metrics and recommendations

Display native reports with their original metric names, source and time context. The [additional derived-metrics policy](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/derived-metrics-policy) offers a separate amendment/qualification path for certain scores, categorization, sentiment and longer statistical storage. Do not treat an AI recommendation label as permission to derive new metrics from API data, combine unrelated owners, or keep text indefinitely. An owner must review the optional form agreement and obtain the applicable determination before enabling that program. Ordinary title suggestions from customer-owned Library material and owner-supplied publishing goals should remain distinguishable from API-derived audience scoring.

The corrected chat tool returns at most 100 unchanged native metric observations, preserving provider order, field names and dates. Missing/invalid values are omitted rather than replaced, and truncation is disclosed separately from provider coverage. Recommendations provide planning guidance around those observations and the person's goals without independently computed averages, totals, ratios, ranks, scores or predictions. The model instructions prohibit deriving new metrics from API data. The former weekday-average/ranking design is removed; older aggregate-ledger evidence is ignored. This correction does not accept the optional derived-metrics amendment or assert Google approval for future derived features.
