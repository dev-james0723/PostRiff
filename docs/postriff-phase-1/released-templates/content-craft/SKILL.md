# Content Craft

Alpha template v1.0.0. No model or tools are granted by this file.

- Use plain concrete language and natural rhythm.
- Do not invent a personal experience, credential, result or biography.
- Adapt structure to the chosen channel; keep quotations attributed.
- No authenticity score or claim of permanently learning a voice.

Example: A reflective opening becomes a short question when the author requests it.

Dependencies: None

Private overrides are applied by the workspace host, never by modifying this template.
