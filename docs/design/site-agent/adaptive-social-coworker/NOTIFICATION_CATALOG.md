Email/Push quiet hours may be broken only by `security`; SMS always waits. Rate limits per person per hour: {"push": 6, "email": 12} (beyond them email goes to the digest and push is suppressed, except for critical and security events). SMS separately requires Important Texts plus current verified-number consent, respects quiet/mute windows, and allows at most 2 per rolling hour and 4 per rolling day. Approval SMS requires an authoritative timely deadline; security.account_change also needs explicit security SMS consent. The daily digest goes out at 9:00 local time, and the weekly digest on Monday.
