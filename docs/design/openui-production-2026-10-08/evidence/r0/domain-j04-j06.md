### 1.5 Learned vs proposed, evidence and confidence
- **Learned (in effect):** `state.learning.active[status=='active']` (paused = listed, not applied); approved voice = `speaker.revisions[activeRevision]` unless `stale`.
- **Proposed (not in effect):** `pr_memory_proposals.status='pending'` (max 3 pending, 30-day TTL, `learning.py:34-37`); `speaker.provisional` (`status 'proposed'|'stale'`).
- **Evidence:** profile `dimensions[]` = `{id ∈ voice_analysis.DIMENSIONS (18), observation (≤240), support[sampleIds], counterEvidence[], evidenceLevel: supported (≥3) | limited | conflicting | insufficient, insufficientEvidence?, quotes[{sourceId,text ≤240}] (AI only, exact-substring verified)}`; `quarantined[]`. Preference items: `evidenceState ∈ user_confirmed | observed_in_approved_example | agent_proposed_needs_confirmation | conflicting`, `evidenceSummary`; proposals `body.evidence[]`, `why`, `support`, `performance` (note only).
- **Confidence:** only `memory_layers._confidence` (`high|medium|not_in_effect`) exists; there is **no numeric confidence** anywhere in J04 **[GAP]** — do not invent one.
- **Wording invariants [V]:** AI proposal unknowns literally say "No model was trained." (`voice_ai.py:108`); local analysis says "provisional until an owner approves it" (`voice_analysis.py:173`).

### 1.6 "Learning job status" — what really exists
- **[V] AI voice analysis is synchronous** inside the `POST /actions` request (no job table, no async status). Observable outcomes: `speaker.provisional.analysisMethod=='ai'` + `contextDigest`; ledger reservation `'voice:'+requestId` settled `completed|failed|unknown`; `pr_ai_call_events` row; audit `voice.analysis_proposed`; or an `AlphaError` 409/422/502.
- **[V] Preference extraction is a cron batch** (`HostedLearning.sweep` → `extract`, `learning_service.py:354-481`, called from the cron route `hosted_app.py:672`). It marks `pr_learning_events.consumed_by` (no `consumed_at`), reserves/settles ledger rows (`provider='learning'`), creates ≤1 automatic proposal/day/workspace.
- **[GAP]** No persisted per-workspace "extraction last ran / outcome" record. A truthful status must be **derived**: unconsumed event count (`SELECT count(*) … consumed_by IS NULL`), newest pending proposal `created_at`, `extractor.kind/allowed`, `learning.enabled`. D must label it derived; never "training"/"trained".
- **[GAP]** No "voice model training" exists at all. Only proposals, approvals and style revisions.
