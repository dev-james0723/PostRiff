# Inbox

The [Inbox](/app/inbox) shows comments on your posts and lets you reply, one at a time.

## Which accounts feed this inbox

Comments appear for accounts whose provider supports comment ingestion and whose comments capability is Direct.

## Replies are individual approvals

You always see the comment in context before writing. A suggestion says what produced it and is never sent on its own: you edit, then approve the exact account, thread and text. There is no bulk reply and no auto-reply, and Rafii never replies from a chat message.

## Who can reply

Replying needs the reply permission (owners, or members with the reply grant).
