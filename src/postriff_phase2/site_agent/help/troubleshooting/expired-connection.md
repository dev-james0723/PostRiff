# An account needs reconnecting

An account card on [Channels](/app/channels) may say Reconnect, Finish setup or Connect instead of "Ready for posting".

## Common causes

- The provider's access expired or was revoked.
- A permission (scope) Rafii needs was not granted.
- The account was disconnected.

## What to do

1. On Channels, choose Re-verify to see what the provider reports now.
2. Choose Reconnect and sign in as the same account when the provider asks. A different account becomes a new card.
3. Posts approved for that account were held while it was disconnected; prepare them again for a new review.

Only members who manage connections (owners, admins or members with that grant) can reconnect. Rafii cannot reconnect an account for you from a chat.
