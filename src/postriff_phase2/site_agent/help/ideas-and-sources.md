# Ideas and sources

[Ideas](/app/ideas) holds the material drafts may draw from: your notes, pasted text, files and links, and pages found by web research.

## Four ways to use a source

- Quote it: your own words, which drafts may quote.
- Rewrite, then approve use: never quoted, and a draft from it is scheduled only after you approve public use of its exact facts.
- Internal only: left out of public drafts.
- Do not use: kept here, left out of every draft.

## Approved facts

Pasted text and files are split into paragraphs. Only the ones you approve can reach a draft; anything else stays out and is listed as unknown rather than guessed.

## Leaving this server

A paid cloud model reads a source's approved facts only when its cloud switch is on. Other writing routes, such as an agent signed in on the machine that runs Rafii, read them without that switch. The text you draft from (an idea, a link, your message) goes to whichever model you pick.

## Web research

When web research is on for the workspace, a draft that needs facts you have not supplied looks them up, and each page read becomes a source here with its address. An owner turns it on or off under [Memory](/app/workspace/memory). Some deployments switch it off entirely.

## Retracting a source

Retracting a source blanks its text and facts. Drafts that used it keep their text but stay blocked until you draft them again.
