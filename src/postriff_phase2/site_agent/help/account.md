# Your account, privacy and data

## Profile and sign-in

[Profile](/app/account/profile) covers your name, picture, sign-in methods and sessions. With two-factor authentication on, every sign-in needs Face ID / Touch ID or a code from your authenticator app. Add a second method as a backup: there are no recovery codes.

## Notifications

[Notifications](/app/account/notifications) chooses which emails and alerts you receive.

## API tokens

[API & integrations](/app/account/api) lists personal tokens that let your own scripts read, draft and propose within your current permissions. Approving, publishing, replying and connecting accounts always stay in the app, with a person.

## Export and diagnostics

On [Privacy & data](/app/account/privacy), an export is a zip of drafts, sources, approvals and receipts, with the SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact file you received. A diagnostics package holds counts and states only, and Rafii does not send it anywhere.

## Deleting the account

Only the workspace owner can delete the account, from Privacy & data. Rafii never deletes anything from a chat message. Publications already handed to a platform must have an outcome first.
