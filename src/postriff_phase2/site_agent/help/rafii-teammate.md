# Working with Rafii in the side panel

Rafii is available from the top bar on every page (or press ⌘J / Ctrl+J). The same conversation follows you from page to page, so you can ask on the Calendar and continue on the Queue.

## What Rafii can do

- Explain the page you are on and what its buttons and states mean.
- Tell you where to find something and open that page for you.
- Look at your workspace (accounts, queue, drafts, automations, memory, plan) and explain why something happened, such as a post that did not publish.
- Write drafts and set up automations, the same way Home does.
- Propose a change to an automation for you to apply with one press.

## What Rafii never does from a chat

Rafii never publishes, replies, sends messages, connects or disconnects accounts, buys credits, deletes anything or reveals passwords, tokens or keys because of a chat message. Those steps stay on their own pages, with you. A plan card, a preview or a "yes" in chat is not an approval.

## What Rafii looked at

Every answer lists what Rafii read (for example "Queue status" or "Help · Approvals") and what it did not read. Answers about Rafii itself link to the help article they come from. When Rafii cannot find an answer it says so instead of guessing.

## Which model answers

Rafii uses the writer you chose on Home and in [Models & providers](/app/account/models). With the preview writer, or when the chosen writer is unavailable or over its budget, Rafii answers straight from help articles and your workspace, and says so. It never switches to a paid model you did not choose.

## Your voice and Rafii's voice

Rafii talks to you in its own plain voice. Posts are still written in your brand voice from your approved voice profile and memory, never in Rafii's chat voice.
