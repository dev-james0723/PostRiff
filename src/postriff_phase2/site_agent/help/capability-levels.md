# Capability levels

Every connected account has a level for each capability (identity, publish, schedule, analytics, comments, reply). Your account's live level always wins over any general description here.

## Direct

Rafii acts through the provider's official API, and only after your approval.

## Assisted

Rafii prepares the post and you complete the last step, for example while the provider's review of Rafii is pending.

## Bridge (Local)

A route through a desktop companion on your own computer. It stays unavailable until the companion release is verified.

## Unsupported

Not offered for this provider yet.

## Why a platform may not publish

Even with an account connected, a post publishes only when every link exists. Rafii names the missing one:

- X and Xiaohongshu: Rafii has no publishing connection yet, so their versions are prepared as drafts for you to post.
- Instagram needs an image, so text-only posts stay drafts.
- Awaiting provider review: the platform has not approved Rafii's publishing access yet.
- Live publishing is not switched on for this deployment, or the provider is paused for maintenance.
- The account is disconnected, needs reconnecting, or is a demo account.
- Your plan does not include publishing right now.

## Help versus your workspace

A help article describes what a platform can do in general. When your account's card says a capability is Unsupported or Assisted, that live state is what applies to you.
