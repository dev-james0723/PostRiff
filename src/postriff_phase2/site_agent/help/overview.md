# Overview

The Overview shows what is scheduled, what needs you, and how much of your plan is left.

## Honest numbers only

Every figure comes from your workspace ledger and publishing receipts. "Unavailable" is never shown as zero.

## What Next up counts

Only approved jobs with a time the worker can read appear there, in your time zone. Drafts waiting for approval are listed under attention instead.

## Nothing publishes without you

Scheduled items only leave the queue after an exact approval of the text, media and time.
