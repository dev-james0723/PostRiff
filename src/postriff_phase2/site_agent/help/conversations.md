# Conversations

Each conversation keeps what you asked and what Rafii produced: drafts for each account, schedule plans, automation cards and answers from the Rafii panel. Recent conversations are listed on Home.

## Drafts in a conversation

A writing run produces candidate drafts, one per account and language. Nothing changes in your workspace until you add them to your drafts. An unedited refresh of a draft you already have stays a proposed update until you accept it.

## Plan cards

When you ask for a post at a time ("post this at 4 PM on LinkedIn"), Rafii adds a plan card. Approving from the card runs the same review and approval chain as the Queue: the exact text, media, account and time are frozen and approved together. The card never publishes by itself.

## Answers from the Rafii panel

Questions you asked in the Rafii side panel appear in the same conversation with the sources Rafii used. Open a conversation in full from the panel's menu.
