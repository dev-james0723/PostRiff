# Calendar

The [Calendar](/app/calendar) shows reviews and publishing jobs, each at its approved time in your time zone.

## What shows here

A review that expired or went out of date, and a job the worker held, stay visible with their own state, so nothing disappears quietly.

## States and filters

Each entry names its state beside its title. The chips above the calendar count the posts in the period on screen; choose one to show only those, and the address keeps the filter for a bookmark.

## Month, week and day

Month shows the shape of the month; week and day place each post on the hour it goes out. Choose a date or "+N more" to open that day. Open an entry to see it in the Queue.

## Moving a post

Timing is part of the exact approval. To change it, prepare the draft again with the new time from [Queue → Drafts](/app/queue?view=drafts).
