# Home

Home is where you start a post. Write an idea, choose where it goes, and Rafii drafts a version for each account.

## Destinations are accounts

Pick the connected accounts, or folders of accounts, a draft is for. Two accounts on one app get two drafts. Channels and times you name in the message still win. A platform with no connected account can still be drafted, but it cannot be published until an account is connected.

## Context Pocket

The agent reads only the sources you included in the Context Pocket and marked usable in [Ideas](/app/ideas); nothing else in your workspace is visible to it while drafting.

## Content type, language, model and voice

- Content type picks the kind of post (for example an announcement or a reflection) and its format.
- Language is chosen per account and remembered per platform.
- Model chooses the writer: Rafii's managed AI model (the default when it is available), a CLI signed in on the machine that serves Rafii, or Templates (no AI model), which writes from templates only when you choose it.
- Writing Voice is neutral, or personalized when you have approved writing samples for the chosen writer.

## Everything is a proposal

Drafts, schedule plans and memory notes are candidates until you approve them. The chat has no way to publish on its own. Save the drafts you like; they appear in [Queue → Drafts](/app/queue?view=drafts).

## Asking for something recurring

If your message asks for something on a schedule ("every Monday, share a practice tip"), Rafii sets it up as an automation instead of drafting once. It tells you what will run when, and whether it waits for your approval. See [Automations](/app/automations).
