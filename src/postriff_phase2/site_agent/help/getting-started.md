# Getting started with Rafii

Rafii turns your ideas into drafts for each of your social accounts, keeps them in one queue, and publishes only what you approve.

## 1. Connect an account

Open [Channels](/app/channels) and connect the account you want to post to. Each account card shows what Rafii has actually verified for it: identity, publishing, scheduling, analytics and comments are separate permissions.

## 2. Write an idea on Home

On [Home](/app), write a thought, paste a paragraph or a link, choose the accounts it is for, and press Generate. Rafii writes one draft per account in the language you chose. Drafts are candidates: nothing is scheduled or published yet.

## 3. Keep the drafts you like

Save the versions that sound like you. They move to the Drafts tab of the [Queue](/app/queue?view=drafts), where you can edit them later.

## 4. Schedule and approve

From Queue → Drafts, choose Schedule…, pick the account and time, and Rafii prepares the exact post for approval. Approving that exact text, media, account and time is the only way a post enters the queue. The worker publishes it at the approved time where the account's publishing is Direct and records a receipt.

## 5. Ask Rafii anywhere

The Rafii button in the top bar opens a panel on every page. Ask what a page does, why something is disabled, why a post did not publish, or ask for a draft or an automation. Rafii shows what it looked at, and it never publishes, replies, connects accounts or spends money from a chat message.
