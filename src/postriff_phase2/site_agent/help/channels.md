# Channels

[Channels](/app/channels) lists the social accounts connected to this workspace and what each one can do.

## Connecting an account

Choose Connect, pick the provider and sign in as the account you want. You need the manage connections permission (owners and admins have it). Rafii never connects an account for you from a chat; the provider's sign-in happens in your browser.

## A connection is not a permission

Identity, publishing, scheduling, analytics and comments are separate grants. Each card shows the level Rafii has actually verified for each, never a blended "Ready". See [Capability levels](/app/help/help_capability_levels).

## What Re-verify checks

Re-verify asks the provider, right now, whether the stored token still identifies the same account and still carries the scopes it was granted. It reports what it found; it does not renew anything.

## Reconnecting

A reconnect repeats the grant for the same account. Sign in as that account when the provider asks; a different account becomes a new card and the old one still needs reconnecting.

## Disconnecting

Stored tokens are wiped and revoked remotely where the provider supports it. Approved jobs for that account are held.

## Folders

Saved folders group accounts you often post to together. Choosing a folder on Home selects its accounts; a folder is a view and never changes an account.
