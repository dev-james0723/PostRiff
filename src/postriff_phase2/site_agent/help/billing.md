# Usage and plan

[Usage & plan](/app/account/billing) shows your plan, what is left of each allowance and the usage ledger.

## Paid work stops at the approved allowance

Before managed-credit work runs, you approve the task's maximum credits. Rafii holds that amount, settles the verified task cost once, and returns unused credits. A failed task charges no customer credits. An unknown cost stays held until it is confirmed; it is never treated as zero. There is no automatic overage charge.

Free has no monthly managed credits. Check Usage & plan for first-value preview availability and your workspace's actual plan and renewal price. Paid subscriptions remain unavailable until commercial approval and payment verification are complete.

## Existing subscriptions and trials

An existing subscription keeps its plan and price. An existing trial keeps its approved one-time allowance until it ends; it does not convert automatically. New workspaces start on Free. Drafts remain readable and exportable when a paid period or trial ends.

## Task availability

A paid task needs a qualified estimate and approval before it runs. If funding or the required credit limit is unavailable, the task does not run and Rafii tells you why. Rafii panel answers never switch to a paid model you did not choose.

## Who manages billing

Owners manage subscriptions and may open the advanced cost diagnostics on Usage & plan. Other members see the plan's customer allowance. Credit top-ups are unavailable until their purchase and legal terms are activated.
