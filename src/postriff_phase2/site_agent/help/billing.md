# Usage and plan

[Usage & plan](/app/account/billing) shows your plan, what is left and the usage ledger. What it shows depends on the plan.

## Free

Free has no monthly credits and no managed writing, image or research allowance. It includes one Post Doctor check and one analysis of up to 20 recent posts. You can still write, edit, plan and export by hand.

## Creator credits

Creator includes 3,500 credits per billing period; 300 credits stand for US$1 of provider cost. Before a paid task runs, Rafii shows the most it can use and waits for you to confirm. You are charged what it actually used, and a task that fails is released. Credits reset each period and do not roll over. When they run out, paid work stops and tells you; nothing is charged silently and there is no automatic overage. Images are not part of plan credits yet.

## Earlier plans: allowances

Earlier plans keep their writing and media allowances. A writing batch is one paid drafting run; runs that use no paid model show at $0 and do not use a batch. Each run reserves an estimate first, then settles to the real cost, or is released if it failed. When an allowance runs out, paid drafting stops and tells you.

## Earlier trials

An earlier trial comes with a one-time allowance and needs no card. Nothing converts automatically; when it ends, you choose a plan yourself. Your drafts stay readable and exportable after a cancellation.

## Spending limits

Every paid model call first reserves its estimated cost against the workspace's budget and stop-line. If the budget is not approved yet or the stop-line would be passed, the call does not run and you are told why. Rafii panel answers never switch to a paid model you did not choose.

## Who sees costs

Only the owner sees money amounts and manages the subscription. Other members see what is left of the plan.
