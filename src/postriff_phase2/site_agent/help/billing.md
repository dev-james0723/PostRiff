# Usage and plan

[Usage & plan](/app/account/billing) shows your plan, what is left of each allowance and the usage ledger.

## Allowances stop, they never overcharge

When a writing or media allowance runs out, paid drafting stops and tells you. Nothing is charged silently and there is no automatic overage.

## What counts as a writing batch

A batch is one paid drafting run. Runs that use no paid model show at $0 and do not use a batch. Each run reserves an estimate first, then settles to the real cost, or is released if it failed.

## Trial

A trial comes with a one-time allowance and needs no card. Nothing converts automatically; when it ends, you choose a plan yourself. Your drafts stay readable and exportable after a cancellation.

## Spending limits

Every paid model call first reserves its estimated cost against the workspace's budget and stop-line. If the budget is not approved yet or the stop-line would be passed, the call does not run and you are told why. Rafii panel answers never switch to a paid model you did not choose.

## Who sees costs

Only the owner sees money amounts and manages the subscription. Other members see allowances.
